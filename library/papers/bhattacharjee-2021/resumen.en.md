The work considers chronology-unprotected mechanisms with very low gravitational polarization so that traversing a wormhole happens with positive energy density everywhere. It relies on Einstein, Dirac and Maxwell fields, with a non-zero stress-energy tensor on spacelike hypersurfaces and a hyperbolic coordinate shift. The approach claims that no exotic matter is needed. It is framed around closed timelike curves in the ADM 3+1 formulation, relevant to traversable wormholes.
