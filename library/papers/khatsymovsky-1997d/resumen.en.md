The work calculates the renormalized expectation values of the stress-energy tensor of a massless fermion vacuum, called the neutrino, in the topology of a static spherically symmetric wormhole. It considers the case where the metric derivatives are small and uses the first orders of a quasiclassical WKB expansion. It finds that the averaged weak energy condition is violated whatever the detailed form of the metric, which is a necessary condition for the neutrino vacuum to support the wormhole. In this respect it resembles the electromagnetic vacuum and differs from the conformal scalar one.
