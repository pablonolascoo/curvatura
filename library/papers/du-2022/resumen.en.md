The paper asks whether time travel is feasible. It first contrasts the absolute and relative views of spacetime, then analyzes seven proposed methods, describing for each its theoretical principles, related theorems, current status and limitations. The method is to collect and integrate published studies and data from space agencies. It concludes that time travel requires not only correct theories but also available technology and energy to carry it out and keep astronauts safe. It serves as an introductory overview of geometries and mechanisms related to exotic propulsion.
