The work simulates Hawking radiation of Reissner-Nordström black holes using the correspondence between a scalar field in curved spacetime and quantum many-body lattice models. In the static background approximation, the 1+1 dimensional field becomes a bosonic hopping Hamiltonian with couplings set by the metric. It is evolved by exact diagonalization and the final state is projected onto exterior modes to get the spectrum. For moderate charge to mass ratios the spectrum agrees very well with a thermal one, and near extremality it remains compatible with larger uncertainty. It points to future analogue implementations with superconducting qubits and trapped ions.
