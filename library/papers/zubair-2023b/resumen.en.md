The article seeks Casimir wormholes in five-dimensional Einstein-Gauss-Bonnet gravity, using Casimir energy as a negative-energy source acting as exotic matter. It incorporates the generalized uncertainty principle through two procedures, KMM and DGS. It builds shape functions, with and without correction, and studies their existence, the effect of the Gauss-Bonnet and minimal-uncertainty parameters on the equation of state, the active gravitational mass and embedding diagrams. It also analyzes the violation of the null energy condition, the equilibrium forces and the complexity factor.
