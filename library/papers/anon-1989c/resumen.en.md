The article discusses relativistic dynamics starting from the assumption that the interval ds^4 is invariant, and from the transformation rules that follow from it. Using the concept of dual velocity, it defines two representations of the motion of matter, a timelike one and a spacelike one. If one assumes that ordinary experiments always observe the timelike representation, a superluminal particle would closely resemble an antiparticle. The work is theoretical and belongs to a series on relativity and faster-than-light motion.
