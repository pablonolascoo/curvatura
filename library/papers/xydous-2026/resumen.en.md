The preprint develops a classical model of the vacuum as a continuum of massless spin carriers and applies it to propellantless propulsion. A spacecraft would impose a standing electromagnetic wave with a time-varying phase gradient; the vacuum would respond with a compensating spin current, and the reaction to that flow would produce thrust without expelling mass. According to the author, the model reproduces reported forces on asymmetric capacitors and suggests that a static phase gradient could reduce effective inertia by forming an inertial bubble. It outlines applications in CubeSat-class probes and a path toward an inertial warp drive.
