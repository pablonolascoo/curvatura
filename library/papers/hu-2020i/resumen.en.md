The chapter summarizes the closed-time-path (in-in) formalism and computes the effective action for a scalar field in Minkowski spacetime. It then treats N quantum matter fields coupled to gravity in an effective field theory approach, and quantizes metric perturbations around a semiclassical background. It gives a prescription for choosing the asymptotic initial vacuum and derives two-point metric correlations using a 1/N expansion, including matter-loop corrections but no graviton loops. A simplified model illustrates the method.
