This work, in Portuguese, starts from the fact that the constancy of the speed of light appears to be a barrier for interstellar journeys, since reaching it would require infinite energy. It explains that in general relativity arbitrarily high global speeds can be achieved without violating it, through warp-type metrics. It studies special and general relativity and some solutions allowing superluminal speeds, with the Alcubierre metric as the main case, the one that inspired the term warp drive.
