The work studies a gauge theory with the non-compact group SO(2,1), whose Killing metric has indefinite signature with two negative signs. It shows that in a static configuration the sector contributions cancel and the energy density vanishes, but with a topological defect, meaning a nontrivial holonomy of the vector potential, the non-compact sector contributes and the energy density becomes negative. The effect is classical and needs no quantization. The author estimates that it scales as the fourth power of the number of magnetic flux quanta divided by R to the fourth, and discusses a laboratory realization in coaxial geometry with resonant excitation and possible cosmological implications.
