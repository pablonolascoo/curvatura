The manuscript summarizes the results of classical physics before quantum mechanics and the hypotheses of classical physicists from the 17th century to the early 21st. It then unifies them into a single coherent picture within the author's Hypersphere World-Universe Model. According to the abstract, the model proposes five types of dark matter particles and predicts their masses, describes the origin, evolution and structure of the world and of macro-objects, and offers a mathematical framework that ties together several fundamental constants and allows their values to be calculated. It is an alternative cosmological proposal with no direct link to propulsion.
