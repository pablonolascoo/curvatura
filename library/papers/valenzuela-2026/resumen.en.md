The authors solve the Dirac equation on a two-dimensional sphere of radius R as an effective equation for charge carriers in buckminsterfullerene. With covariant derivatives and spin connection they obtain electronic states in terms of Jacobi polynomials. They construct the Ritus functions, which allow the Dirac propagator in curved momentum space to be expressed through a flat free propagator. They compute charge and current densities and compare them with the flat case as R tends to infinity. The framework extends to other curved spaces.
