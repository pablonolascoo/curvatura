The article uses the hypothetical activities of highly advanced cultures, especially space and time travel, to examine basic questions in physics. It considers Hawking's chronology protection conjecture applied to wormholes and the nature of time in several interpretations of quantum mechanics. It presents a transient inertial reaction effect that would make it possible, fleetingly, to concentrate large amounts of negative mass-energy. It points out that if the strong version of Mach's principle is correct, forming wormholes with negative mass would be impossible.
