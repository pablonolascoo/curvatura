This thesis studies analogue stochastic gravity in strongly interacting Bose-Einstein condensates. It starts from the idea, with a long history in fluids, crystals with defects and quantum entanglement, that curved spacetime is an emergent structure, something that remains speculative as an explanation of gravitation itself. Condensates serve as a laboratory to reproduce effects of quantum fields in curved geometries and their fluctuations. It belongs to analogue gravity and is useful for testing ideas about horizons in the laboratory.
