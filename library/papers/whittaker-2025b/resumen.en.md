This dissertation proposes a unified theoretical framework that reformulates physics without time in an energy space, and introduces the Curvature-Aligned Transport System, a model of deterministic spatial transitions that do not rely on classical displacement, velocity or time evolution. Motion would arise from entropy-gradient-driven flows in a curved energy manifold, formulated with tensor calculus and non-temporal variational principles. The author states that simulations validate the model and that scenarios such as repositioning an object between Earth and Moon would be feasible with structured electromagnetic fields. It is a highly speculative proposal with an experimental roadmap.
