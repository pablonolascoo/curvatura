This article, written from the relationship between science and theology, is the second part of a series on the border between them. It argues that explanations of natural phenomena, such as the Pauli exclusion principle, Einstein's spacetime or the apparent paradoxes of quantum mechanics, move the limit of our knowledge toward metaphysics. According to the author, the writings of philosophers from Aristotle and Aquinas to Kant do not conflict with the ontological conclusions of modern physics. It is a philosophical and theological essay, with no calculations and no direct link to propulsion.
