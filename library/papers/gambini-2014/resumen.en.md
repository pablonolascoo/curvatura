The authors study Hawking radiation in a spherical black hole of loop quantum gravity. They start from the exact quantum solution of the vacuum with spherical symmetry and propagate a test scalar field on it, using quantum field theory techniques on quantum spacetimes. The discreteness of area modifies the quantum vacua and removes the trans-Planckian modes near the horizon, so singularities disappear from quantities such as the stress-energy tensor. The Boulware, Hartle-Hawking and Unruh vacua change little, and the Hawking radiation comes out like the conventional one but with a high-frequency cutoff.
