The author considers a toy model of the large-scale universe with generalized holographic dark energy with a Nojiri-Odintsov cut-off, accompanied by an inhomogeneous viscous dark fluid. Besides a cosmographic analysis, he applies the Om and two-point Om diagnostics and estimates present-day values of the statefinder parameters. He also studies the validity of the generalized second law of thermodynamics and states that the theoretical results agree well with observational data, with constraints from Planck 2015.
