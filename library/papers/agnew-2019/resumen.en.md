This work surveys the theory and technology of warp drives to judge whether pursuing them is feasible. It has two aims: to encourage interest in technologies that look impossible today, and to provide a roadmap for newcomers to the field. The tour runs from the theoretical side of spacetime metrics to applied experiments, including interferometry and electromagnetics. It points out the weaknesses and the gaps in knowledge and technology that would have to be closed. The author concludes that progress will need coordinated teamwork across several scientific and engineering disciplines.
