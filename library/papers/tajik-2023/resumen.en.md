The authors investigate signal propagation in a quantum field simulator of the Klein-Gordon model, made from two strongly coupled parallel one-dimensional quasi-condensates. After a quench, they measure local phononic fields and observe correlations travelling along sharp light-cone fronts. When the atomic density is inhomogeneous, the fronts are curved. The measured velocity agrees with predictions based on geodesics of an inhomogeneous metric, extending quantum simulations to general spacetime metrics.
