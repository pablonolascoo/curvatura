The authors show that, in the near-horizon static isotropic metric, all massless field equations of spin up to 2 reduce to a Fuchsian-type equation with three regular singular points. They obtain three general solutions depending on parameters, two of them with discrete imaginary frequencies. From this they derive exact formulas for the quasinormal modes of Rindler-type spacetime and a wave equation that serves as the basis of perturbation theory in static spherically symmetric spacetimes.
