This book chapter, titled Stepping Stone, is a popular essay on space exploration and why humanity should leave Earth. It starts from quotations by Seneca and Tsiolkovsky to present space as a wild, indifferent territory full of dangers. It argues that the decision to go says as much about human nature as about space itself, and that we may have more than one chance to ascend, which could lead us to procrastinate. It contains no calculations or physical results.
