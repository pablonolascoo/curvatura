The authors examine whether superluminal warp drives would survive the switching on of quantum effects, even if the required exotic matter existed. They study the behaviour of the renormalized stress-energy tensor in the associated spacetimes. They find it grows exponentially in time near the front wall of the superluminal bubble. They conclude these geometries are unstable against semiclassical back-reaction, a serious argument against the physical viability of superluminal warp drives.
