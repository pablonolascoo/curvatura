The article analyzes a ground-state atom uniformly accelerated through the Minkowski vacuum, which can become excited by emitting an Unruh-Minkowski photon. The authors show that, from the accelerated atom's viewpoint, the frequency of those photons can be positive or negative depending on the acceleration direction. It becomes excited by emitting a photon of negative frequency in its frame and decays by emitting one of positive frequency. A photon emitted this way cannot be absorbed by another ground-state atom accelerating in the same direction, but it can be absorbed by an excited one or one accelerating the opposite way. Similar effects appear in Cherenkov radiation.
