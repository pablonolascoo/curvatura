The work studies rotational superradiance, in which a wave scattered by a rotating black hole is amplified by extracting energy. Because it is very hard to observe in astrophysics, laboratory analogues are used, but existing ones require external sources. Here a coaxial twin rotor system that stimulates itself is found: one rotor emits acoustic waves carrying orbital angular momentum toward the vortex airflow generated by the other. Experiments show the reflected and transmitted waves are amplified when the vortex-generating rotor spins faster than the emitting one. It is an acoustic analogue of superradiance, with possible use in turbofans.
