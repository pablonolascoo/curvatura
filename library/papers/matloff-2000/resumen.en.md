The chapter reviews current approaches to interstellar flight and points out that all of them have problems. Solar sails are feasible but need millennia of travel, nuclear pulse would require storing large amounts of nuclear material near Earth, antimatter is very fast but expensive, and ramjets would only be realistic in the longer term. The laser light sail, favoured by some authors, requires aiming a beam across trillions of kilometres and sustaining support for decades. Hence the interest in exotic possibilities.
