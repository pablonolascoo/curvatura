The authors establish analogues of the Hawking and Penrose singularity theorems under weaker energy conditions: energy averages with exponential damping and local averages of the stress-energy tensor inspired by quantum energy inequalities. As applications they prove singularity theorems for the Einstein equations with a classical scalar field that violates the strong energy condition and with the nonminimally coupled scalar field, which also violates the null energy condition. It is relevant to what negative energy the singularity theorems allow.
