The work investigates the possibility of shielding gravity and its relation to time dilation. It recalls that since the 19th century various experiments have tried to understand gravitational shielding, with some failing to prove it and others claiming it possible. The authors state that, if proved, it would be possible to travel across black holes or large masses by defying gravity. They study the history of shielding and consider advanced spacecraft or other warp drive mechanisms with suitable shielding. It is a speculative review that acknowledges the complexity of the problem.
