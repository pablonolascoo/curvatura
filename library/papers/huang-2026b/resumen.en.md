The work records in real time how a stationary GHz harmonic mode-locked regime forms in a fibre laser hosting over a hundred intracavity solitons. It uses dispersive Fourier transform and high-bandwidth detection. It distinguishes two stages: a rapid soliton generation phase driven by nonlinear effects, and a long self-organisation phase in which repulsion from gain depletion and recovery equalises the spacing. It is nonlinear optics with no relation to propulsion.
