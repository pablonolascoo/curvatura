The article starts from the fact that many theoretical papers require exotic matter with average negative energy for wormholes and warp drives, yet there is little hope that it exists. Since scalar fields non-minimally coupled to gravity appear to be the current mathematical direction, it proposes that exotic matter is really scalar field interaction. To this end it investigates the Ginzburg-Landau scalar fields associated with superconducting junctions as a source of negative vacuum energy fluctuations, with which to study the interplay of fluctuations, cosmological scalar (Higgs) fields and gravity.
