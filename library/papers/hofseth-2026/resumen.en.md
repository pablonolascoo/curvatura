The report presents a theoretical and engineering specification of a propulsion system called the Scalar-Hydraulic Drive. It builds on refractive vacuum gravity, which treats the vacuum as a medium with a variable refractive index modulated by a scalar field coupled to the trace anomaly of the energy-momentum tensor. It identifies the 95.4 GeV di-photon resonance, seen at the LHC with a combined local significance of 3.1 sigma, as the dilaton or radion mediator. The mechanism uses intense magnetic gradients to raise the index and create a metric bubble that would allow hypersonic transport above Mach 26. It is a speculative metric engineering proposal.
