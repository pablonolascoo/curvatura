This book addresses the goal of advanced propulsion: creating the exotic materials and technologies needed for stargates and warp drives, or at least accelerating a spaceship without carrying a huge fuel reservoir. It has three parts. The first sets out the relativity needed to understand the effects that would enable such propulsion and the proposed techniques. The second collects experimental investigations into whether those effects exist and can be applied. The third, the most speculative, examines what physics would be needed to build wormholes and warp drives, whether it is plausible and how they might be built. It gathers and updates material from various sources.
