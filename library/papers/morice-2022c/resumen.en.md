Review report on a study of wave packet dynamics and eigenstate localization in generalized lattice models whose low-energy behaviour mimics a quantum field theory in 1+1 curved spacetime, aiming to create black hole analogues. It observes a critical slowdown of zero-energy wave packets with power-law hopping, indicating a horizon, while packets of non-zero energy bounce back before reaching it. A semiclassical description coincides with the geodesics of the effective metric.
