The work starts from the fact that hydrodynamic vortices are now used to build laboratory models of black holes, so-called analogue black holes, and that gravitomagnetic effects in these models remain unexplored. The authors focus on the experimental development of a hydrodynamic vortex flow in a magnetic fluid, driven by the continuous rotation of a rare earth magnet. Micro-vortices under rotating magnetic fields had been studied, but not the formation of hydrodynamic vortices. It opens a path for studying magnetically modulated analogue black holes.
