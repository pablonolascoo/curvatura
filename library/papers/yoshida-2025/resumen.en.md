The authors start from the fact that negative energy is key to new space propulsion technologies and was believed impossible to generate by electromagnetic means. They theoretically demonstrate a mutual induction circuit that transiently separates zero energy into positive and negative energy, through rapid cancellation of magnetic flux when coils are connected in series and then differentially. The separated amounts are equal, so energy conservation is respected. They optimize the separation efficiency and find a maximum net efficiency of 367% at a coupling coefficient of 0.992. Experiments agree well with theory and simulation.
