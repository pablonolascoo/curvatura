This book chapter introduces black hole physics as the gravitational collapse of an imploding massive body. It stresses that the possible detection of supermassive black holes at galactic centers makes these studies especially relevant. It also points to a historical survey and a review of current research. According to the available sources, it is an introductory text without new results. It provides a theoretical background for horizon and general relativity topics that appear in wormholes and warp drives.
