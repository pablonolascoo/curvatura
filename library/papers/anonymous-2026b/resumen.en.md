The study numerically computes momentum-space two-point correlations for the Hawking effect in a transcritical quantum fluid, using the truncated Wigner approximation on a realistic driven-dissipative polariton system. It resolves the Hawking-partner and greybody-factor channels by frequency and finds both have comparable strength, so the well-known real-space moustache pattern is an interference between them. The Hawking-partner channel shows negative correlations, a signature of quantum-vacuum pair creation, with amplitudes detectable in current experiments.
