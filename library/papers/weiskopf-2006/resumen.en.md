This application paper reports on more than fifteen years of experience in relativistic and astrophysical visualization, culminating in the Einstein Year 2005. It focuses on explanatory and illustrative visualizations to communicate special and general relativity, their geometric structure, cosmology and astrophysics, with strategies based on physics and mathematics didactics. It describes which methods work in different media, such as magazines, television, talks or interactive museum installations. It contributes technical novelties such as GPU-based special relativistic rendering, general relativistic ray tracing with multiple charts and interactive gravitational light deflection.
