This opinion piece argues that Einstein deserves the 2013 Nobel Prize in Physics for explaining how particles acquire mass, rather than Englert and Higgs. The author believes CERN only confirmed the particle's existence, not its role in giving mass. He accepts vacuum fluctuations but interprets them as binary digits flipping between 1 and 0, and proposes a nonlinear time for the origin of the universe. It is a personal proposal, with no calculations or data in the sources.
