This text is a piece of science fiction set in the year 2328, in the double star system BD-05 1844, or Gliese 250, 28.4 light years from the Sun. It describes the two stars: a K3V orange dwarf with about 80 percent of the solar mass and only 14.6 percent of its luminosity, and an M2.5V red dwarf with half the solar mass. Their separation is around 500 astronomical units. It serves as a setting to imagine a spacefaring civilisation, with no new calculations or physical results.
