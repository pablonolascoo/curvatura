The author presents a general axially symmetric Petrov type II spacetime that violates causality, because it allows closed timelike curves to form after a definite instant. The metric admits an expansion-free, twist-free and shear-free null geodesic congruence. From the general metric he obtains two particular type II cases: a vacuum solution and a Ricci-flat one with a negative cosmological constant. It is an exact example of a geometric time machine relevant to the study of chronology protection.
