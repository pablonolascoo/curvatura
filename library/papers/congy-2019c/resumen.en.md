A new type of wave-mean flow interaction is identified and studied: a small-amplitude linear modulated wave propagates through an evolving nonlinear fluid state, such as a rarefaction wave or a dispersive shock. Using the Korteweg-de Vries equation as an example, the authors derive modulation equations and two adiabatic invariants that decide whether the wavepacket is transmitted or trapped. The predictions agree with numerical simulations and do not require integrability, so they extend to other dispersive models.
