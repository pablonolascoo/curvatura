The essay investigates whether matter-vacuum coupling can avoid the need for exotic matter violating the null energy condition in traversable topological bridges. Focusing on static, zero-tidal-force solutions, it proves a no-go theorem: such coupling does not remove the violation. The geometric flare-out condition is incompatible with sources that respect the null energy condition, whatever the coupling or equation of state, and interaction gradients obstruct the geometry. The author reads this as causality protection built into the field equations themselves.
