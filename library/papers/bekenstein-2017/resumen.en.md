The work presents three-dimensional nanophotonic structures designed with ideas from general relativity, so that light evolves according to the curvature of the medium's space. As a demonstration, light is studied in a paraboloid structure inspired by the Schwarzschild metric of a massive black hole. The design allows control over trajectories, diffraction and the phase and group velocities of wave packets. The structure also shows tunnelling through an electromagnetic bottleneck. It is an optical emulation of curved space, with applications to integrated photonic circuits.
