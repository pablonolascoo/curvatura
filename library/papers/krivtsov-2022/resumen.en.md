The author proposes an analogy between mass transfer in space and energy transfer in solids. He adapts the classical equations of matter dynamics to describe energy dynamics, with effective mass, momentum and moment of inertia for bodies of energy. Two new concepts are introduced: the carrier, a medium enabling transport, and the phantom, a virtual body whose mass distribution equals the energy distribution. Using an inhomogeneous chain of particles, he shows that the phantom obeys Newton's second law and, depending on the ratio of transfer and dispersion velocities, behaves as a wave or a particle. He points to uses in quantum mechanics, electrodynamics and general relativity.
