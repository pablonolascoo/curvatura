The chapter argues that time-asymmetric spacetime structures, such as black holes and the expansion of the universe, are tied to other arrows of time, such as the second law and the retardation of radiation. The nature of the quantum arrow, often attributed to wave function collapse, is essential for understanding the black hole information loss paradox. This paradox takes a new form and may be avoided in a consistent causal treatment that dispenses with horizons and singularities. The master arrow might even reverse direction with respect to a fundamental physical clock such as cosmic expansion.
