The article studies how waves can transmit energy over long distances in an inhomogeneous medium arranged so that the wave propagates with almost no reflection or scattering. Using the Laplace cascade method for second order hyperbolic equations, it proposes a general algorithm to find the parameters of nonuniform reflectionless flows. It applies this to long linear surface waves in a channel with variable cross section, illustrates the analysis with several representative solutions and compares them with earlier studies. It could help reduce the impact of waves on ships, marine structures and coastal activity. It has no direct link to exotic propulsion.
