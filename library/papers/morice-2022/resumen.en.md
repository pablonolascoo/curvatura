The authors study wave packet dynamics and eigenstate localization in generalized lattice models whose low energy dynamics mimics a quantum field theory in 1+1 dimensional curved spacetime, aiming to create black hole analogues. In tight binding models with power law hopping, zero energy wave packets slow down critically, a sign of a horizon, while packets with nonzero energy bounce back before reaching it. All eigenstates localize with a power law, and the forbidden regions set the closest approach to the horizon. A semiclassical description confirms that the trajectories coincide with geodesics of the effective metric.
