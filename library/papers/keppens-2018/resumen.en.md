The work starts from the fact that attempts at an emergent quantum theory of gravity need spacetime to be discretized at the Planck scale, and conjectures that reality is a sub-quantum dynamics of micro-constituents linked by a single interaction law. To build a complex-system toy model, it combines two strategies: viewing gravity as an entropic phenomenon and generalizing the dimensional reduction of the holographic principle to relate the universal constants to attributes of those constituents, and deriving the interaction law from a solution of Einstein's equations. It proposes a Planck-scale origin for black hole thermodynamics.
