This is an assessment of the make-or-break issues of faster-than-light spacetimes, such as traversable wormholes and warp drives. It explains that these geometries require exotic matter in general relativity, and that the argument that violating the energy conditions makes them implausible turns out to be spurious. The real technical challenge would be identifying, quantifying and producing that matter. It also reviews causality problems and the constraints attributed to quantum effects, and summarizes the present state of their resolution.
