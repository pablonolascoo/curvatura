The study analyzes static spherical traversable wormholes in modified f(R,T) gravity, with the model f = R + alpha R squared + lambda T and a family of Morris-Thorne shape functions with exponent n between 0 and 1, which includes the usual case as n = 1/2. The family satisfies the throat, flare-out and asymptotic flatness conditions by construction. The effective null energy condition is violated in the radial direction throughout the region, but in the matter sector only near the throat, with an analytic transition radius. It also derives the force balance from the generalized TOV equation and clarifies the role of each parameter.
