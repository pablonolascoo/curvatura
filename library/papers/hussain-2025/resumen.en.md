The paper proposes a theory of relativity valid for all velocities, including those above the speed of light, based on a single transformation law for four-vectors and tensors under a universal rotation matrix. It claims to satisfy the principle of relativity and the conservation laws, and to give form invariance of spacetime laws. It applies the method to electrodynamic laws with two calculation techniques, which agree except on the conservation law, and arrives at a structure it calls zero-point electrodynamics. It states the model is also valid in noninertial frames.
