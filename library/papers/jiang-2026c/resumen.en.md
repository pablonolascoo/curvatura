The authors investigate whether the renormalized stress-energy tensor of a non-minimally coupled massive scalar field in a zero-tidal wormhole can satisfy the Morris-Thorne conditions for traversability. They compute the tensor with Hadamard renormalization and the Levi-Ori mode-sum method, varying the field mass and the coupling constant. They find three disconnected regions of parameter space that satisfy the conditions and two mass intervals where they cannot be met for any coupling, which sets mass exclusion regions for building traversable wormholes.
