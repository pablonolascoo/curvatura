This is a review, in Chinese, of dynamic vibration absorbers based on acoustic black holes attached to a structure, which avoid weakening the load bearing section of the main part. The authors build a modal coupling model of the combined system and explain how absorption and extra damping arise. They classify the designs as one dimensional (beam or spiral types) and two dimensional (disk, eccentric, slotted types) and summarize uses in aerospace panels, ship floating rafts and high speed train bodies. They conclude that the devices suppress vibration over a wide frequency range with little added mass. It has no direct link to warp propulsion.
