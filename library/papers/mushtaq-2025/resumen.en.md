The work examines the weak deflection angle, shadow, Regge-Wheeler potential and greybody factor of a regular black hole with a multi-polytropic equation of state. In the weak field limit they compute light deflection with the Gauss-Bonnet theorem and the Gibbons and Werner method, in plasma and non-plasma media. They also study the shadow and the spin dependent Regge-Wheeler potential, which governs perturbations, and rigorously bound the greybody factor. It is a black hole analysis with no direct link to warp propulsion.
