The work studies the probability distribution of individual measurements of the smeared energy density of quantum fields in the Minkowski vacuum, in two and four dimensions. These distributions have a finite negative lower bound but no upper bound, so arbitrarily large positive fluctuations are possible. In two dimensions an exact unique analytic form is obtained. In four dimensions the first 65 moments are used to estimate the lower bound and the asymptotic form of the tail, with the caveat that the moments do not determine the distribution uniquely. It is applied to estimate nucleation rates of black holes and Boltzmann brains. It is useful for bounding negative energy.
