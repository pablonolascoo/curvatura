The article develops the 5D World-Universe Model, based on a Medium of the World made of massive particles, among them protons, electrons, photons, neutrinos and dark matter. It treats several aspects of gravitation, such as the measured value of Newton's constant, gravitational lensing, cosmological redshift, light deflection and a gravitational refraction it proposes. It claims to show the interconnection of cosmological parameters and to give a mathematical framework for computing them from the gravitational constant. It concludes that the Medium must exist and that gravity, space and time are emergent phenomena. It is an alternative, speculative proposal.
