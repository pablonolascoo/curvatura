This thesis studies causality properties of asymptotically anti-de Sitter spacetimes and looks for them in the dual conformal field theory, within the AdS/CFT correspondence. After reviewing the correspondence and some puzzles around the Ryu-Takayanagi conjecture, it shows that boundary causality tied to the Gao-Wald theorem implies holographic CFTs on Minkowski space satisfy the averaged null energy condition, which requires positive integrated null energy along a null line. Violating it would give causal shortcuts through the bulk, an issue linked to negative energy and superluminal travel.
