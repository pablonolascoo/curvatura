We prove a sharp quantitative extension of Llarull's theorem to complete noncompact spin manifolds. Let $Φ:M\to\mathbf S^m$ be an area-decreasing map of nonzero degree that is locally constant at infinity, and assume that $\operatorname{scal}_{g_M}\geq m(m-1)$ on $\operatorname{supp}(dΦ)$. If $λ_1(M,g_M)=0$, then $\inf_M\operatorname{scal}_{g_M}<0$; if $λ_1(M,g_M)>0$, then \[ \inf_M\operatorname{scal}_{g_M}\leq -\frac{4m}{m-1}λ_1(M,g_M). \] The coefficient is sharp in every dimension, as shown by an explicit family of warped metrics. We also prove a boundary analogue under the same support-localized scalar-curvature hypothesis, without requiring the strict scalar-curvature positivity condition near the boundary used in Liu--Liu's doubling argument. Applications include a Geroch-type obstruction for connected sums with compact area-enlargeable manifolds and a Ricci-flat splitting theorem for complete metrics with nonnegative scalar curvature and mean-convex boundary.
