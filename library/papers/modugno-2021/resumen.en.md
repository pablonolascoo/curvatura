This is a short commentary in Nature Physics on table-top superfluid experiments that bring the physics of astrophysical black holes into the lab. It points out that the presence of two event horizons in these superfluid black holes complicates matters and makes them more interesting. The keywords mention superfluid helium, Hawking radiation and quantum gravity. It is an explanatory text on analogue black holes, with no results of its own described in the sources.
