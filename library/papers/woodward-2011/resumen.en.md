The article starts from Morris and Thorne's 1988 result that a traversable wormhole would need about a Jupiter mass of negative-mass matter in a structure a few tens of meters across, which seemed unachievable. The author inverts the question: if very advanced beings had built stargates, what must be true of the laws of physics? Elementary arithmetic indicates an exotic density of order 10^22 g/cm3, far above nuclear density, which would also have to be achieved with low energies.
