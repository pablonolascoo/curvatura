The authors propose a ring-shaped, two-dimensional atomic Bose-Einstein condensate as an analogue model to study back-reaction during the pre-heating of the early universe. They analyse the out-of-equilibrium dynamics in which the inflaton field decays by parametrically exciting vacuum fluctuations of matter fields. Using numerical simulations in the truncated Wigner approximation, they find that back-reaction beyond the semiclassical level shows up as an effective friction on the inflaton analogue, as spatial de-phasing of its oscillations, and as entanglement between inflaton and matter fields.
