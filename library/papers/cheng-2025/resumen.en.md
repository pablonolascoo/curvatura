The article presents two proposed principles, Mule's Third and Fourth Laws. The third claims that only particles of similar size interact, according to the ratio of their dual factors, and from this explains dark matter as particles from other spatial dimensions. The fourth proposes endless unit-particle spaces forming the structure of the universe. It introduces an intermediate factor theory as a route to traverse spacetime and communicate between parallel spaces, with speculative applications such as superluminal travel. The sources show no calculations or data supporting these claims.
