This thesis, in French, analyses how quantum correlations are created and destroyed in cosmological inflation and in an analogue preheating experiment. It starts from the fact that inflation amplifies initial quantum fluctuations and that, during preheating, a strong classical field parametrically excites quantum fields and produces entangled particles. When the source is the metric itself, one works with quantum field theory in curved spacetime. The study follows the evolution of entanglement and decoherence, and uses analogue gravity to reproduce these processes in the laboratory.
