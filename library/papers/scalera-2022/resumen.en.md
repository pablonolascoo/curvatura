The article defends a non-Newtonian view of the universe based on hydrodynamic gravitation and an expanding Earth, with the aether as protagonist. It starts from Earth science observations and the Borexino and KamLAND geoneutrino experiments, and computes aether density, flow rate and velocity with the help of astrophysics. It claims that cosmological and gravitational redshift share a single cause, and that the aether reaches a superluminal speed at the Earth's surface, citing INFN experiments. It concludes that current physics and cosmology would need revision. It is a heterodox proposal that is not generally accepted.
