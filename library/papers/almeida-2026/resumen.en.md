The paper analyses the low-energy spectrum of one-dimensional Dirac systems whose velocity vanishes at a point, v(x) proportional to |x|^alpha, together with a domain-wall mass. For alpha different from 1 the first excited-state energy follows an algebraic law, confirmed by numerical lattice diagonalisation. The linear case is different: it reduces to a Morse potential and supports a single bound state, the Jackiw-Rebbi zero mode, with no intrinsic excitation gap. The excitation energy obeys a universal scaling law with Lambert-W structure. Its link to the topic is indirect, since it resembles analogue horizons.
