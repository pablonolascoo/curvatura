This chapter covers zeta-function regularisation for quantum fields in curved space, an elegant method tied to the heat kernel, though limited to Euclidean spaces with enough symmetry for the spectrum to be computed. It first defines the zeta function, shows how to compute it in several spacetimes and relates it to the heat kernel and to the effective Lagrangian through Schwinger proper time. It then obtains the correlation function of the stress-energy bitensor, the noise kernel, from the second metric variation of the effective action. That kernel is central to stochastic gravity.
