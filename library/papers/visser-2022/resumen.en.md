The authors ask what happens when a finite-mass payload is put inside a warp bubble, which forces them to specify what a warp bubble is, what it means for it to "move" relative to the fixed stars, and what mass means in these spacetimes. They work with the Natário zero-vorticity warp drive and derive a suitable line element for a bubble carrying a payload. The resulting spacetime allows an exact and explicit computation of all stress-energy components. They confirm that the null, weak, strong and dominant energy conditions are violated.
