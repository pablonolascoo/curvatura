This essay, written by someone without formal training in theoretical physics, discusses warp drives and wormholes and asks whether they could be combined into one unified system. The idea stems from the problems any practical application of both concepts faces for superluminal travel. The central question is whether a warp bubble could travel through a wormhole. The text offers no calculations or results, only open questions, and asks for comments from specialists in general relativity. It is an exploratory reflection on joining two proposals for faster-than-light travel.
