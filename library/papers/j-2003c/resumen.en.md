The work derives quantum weak energy inequalities for the electromagnetic and massive spin-one fields in globally hyperbolic spacetimes with compact Cauchy surfaces and trivial first homology group. They give state-independent lower bounds on weighted averages of the renormalised energy density measured along any timelike trajectory, valid for Hadamard states. The bound takes a simple form along static trajectories in ultrastatic spacetimes, such as Minkowski space and the static Einstein universe. It matters because it limits the negative energy available.
