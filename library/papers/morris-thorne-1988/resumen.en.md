This classic paper presents rapid interstellar travel through wormholes as a tool for teaching elementary general relativity, with a nod to Carl Sagan's novel Contact. It gives several objections to using black holes or Schwarzschild wormholes, and presents a new class of solutions of the Einstein equations describing wormholes traversable by humans. These require a throat with no horizon, which imposes an enormous radial tension on the material that exceeds its energy density and violates all the energy conditions. No known material does this, but quantum field theory hints it might be possible.
