This epilogue places semiclassical and stochastic gravity in perspective relative to quantum gravity, understood as a theory of the microscopic structure of spacetime, not necessarily obtained by quantizing general relativity. It distinguishes a top-down approach from the Planck energy, which includes string theory and amounts to emergent gravity, from a more conservative bottom-up approach, that of stochastic gravity. It warns against assuming that quantizing relativity yields the microscopic structure. It mentions kinetic theory and effective theories using large N techniques.
