In certain nonrelativistic condensed matter models, sound obeys a relativistic wave equation. The authors ask whether devices exist that would experience that relativity and describe a thought experiment with "acoustic observers" who use sound clocks connected in chains. They conclude that stationary and moving chains perceive each other as undergoing length contraction and time dilation by the Lorentz factor, with c equal to the speed of sound. Observers restricted to sound clocks describe a universe kinematically consistent with special relativity, despite the laboratory's preferred frame. The clocks could be used to probe analogue relativity models.
