This thesis explores geometric aspects of general relativity without explicitly solving Einstein's equations. It builds a preferred coordinate system from the Kodama vector, generalizes surface gravity in two ways and uses it to bound the entropy of static and rotating objects without horizons. It also develops improved versions of the Raychaudhuri equation and studies quantum inequalities. It provides a variational proof of one version of the quantum interest conjecture in 3+1 dimensional Minkowski space, a result that constrains how negative energy density must be repaid by positive energy.
