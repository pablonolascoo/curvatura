The authors present a geometry designed to fit the popular description of a "time machine": a box that lets those inside travel backwards and forwards through time and space, as seen by an external observer. Observers travel inside a bubble that follows a circular acausal trajectory, and if they keep a persistent acceleration their worldlines close. They analyze the causal structure and physicality of the model, which makes spacetime non-orientable, is geodesically incomplete, has naked singularities and requires exotic matter.
