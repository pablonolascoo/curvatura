The authors use quantum field theory on quantum spacetimes to characterize the quantum vacua of a spherical black hole in loop quantum gravity and compute the Hawking radiation. They start from an exact quantum solution of the vacuum Einstein equations and propagate a spherically symmetric test scalar field. The discreteness of area removes trans-Planckian modes near the horizon and with them the singularities in the stress-energy tensor. The Boulware, Hartle-Hawking and Unruh vacua change little. The Hawking radiation comes out similar to the conventional one, but with a high-frequency cutoff.
