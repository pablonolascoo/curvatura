The article envisions rocket-free space propulsion based on the coupling between gravity, electromagnetism and spacetime, called space coupling propulsion. It notes that new theories and observations about the properties of space offer fresh approaches to consider this possibility. To guide the search, evaluation and application of these ideas, it presents several hypothetical mechanisms that highlight the requirements that would have to be met for such a breakthrough. It also gives a brief introduction to the emerging opportunities. It is a framing text without experimental results, a starting point for NASA's breakthrough propulsion physics efforts.
