Using numerical simulations, the work proposes an optical black hole laser: an amplifier formed by travelling refractive index perturbations that trap light between a white and a black hole horizon. The simulations show the growth of positive and negative frequency modes inside the cavity, with a weaker emission in periodic bursts. A new regime also appears in which the spectrum broadens until it reaches the zero-frequency points, where amplification is highest, and for long times only those modes lase. It is an optical analogue of horizons.
