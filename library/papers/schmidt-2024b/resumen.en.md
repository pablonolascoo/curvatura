The work proposes an analog model for the dynamics of a charged scalar particle in the background of a Kerr-Sen black hole, whose metric comes from string theory. The model consists of a particle constrained to move on a spherical surface that may rotate and be under an electromagnetic field. The authors show that this particle reproduces exactly the dynamics of a dilaton in the Kerr-Sen space. The functions describing the dynamics are written as confluent Heun functions, and they match those of the original system. It is an example of analogue gravity.
