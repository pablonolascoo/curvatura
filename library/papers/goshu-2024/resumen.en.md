The article studies how quantum gravity affects so-called gravitational rainbows, meaning the bending of light around massive objects. It combines theoretical models, experimental findings and computer simulations to analyze how gravitational potentials and light-beam paths change. According to the abstract, quantum gravitational effects show up in how the gravitational potential spreads and in the distribution of perturbations, with small variations in the z direction. It belongs to quantum gravity and gravitational optics, with an indirect link to propulsion.
