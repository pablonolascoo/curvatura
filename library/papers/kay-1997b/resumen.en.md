The work proves two theorems on the difficulties of formulating a quantum linear scalar field theory on a spacetime with a compactly generated Cauchy horizon. They show that the theory breaks down at certain base points of the horizon. The first says that the usual field algebra has no extension satisfying F-locality at any base point. The second says that the two-point distribution of any Hadamard state must be singular at every base point. The authors interpret this as support for Hawking's chronology protection conjecture, according to which the laws of physics prevent building a time machine.
