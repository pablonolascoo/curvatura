This chapter of the book The Starship Engineer compares the parameters of nuclear pulse engines depending on spacecraft design and mission type. It contrasts a 100-day Mars mission with a 100-year interstellar mission, noting that the engine performance required is very different in each case. In general, an interstellar trip demands propulsion many orders of magnitude more demanding than an interplanetary one. This is why interstellar flight is so much harder. It is a spacecraft engineering text, not exotic physics.
