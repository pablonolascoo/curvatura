The article sets out the basic science that would serve as a background for a speculative gravitational propulsion technology. It applies generalised functions to the formalism of general relativity to introduce finite dynamic intervals of spacetime, which would be warped by acceleration solitons. These solitons are interpreted as causing geodesic deviations in the spacetime continuum. It relates to warp-drive spacetimes. The text is theoretical and gives no numerical results in its summary.
