Using simple kinematic arguments, the author derives the Fokker-Planck equation for diffusion processes in curved spacetimes. For Brownian motion it coincides with Eckart's relativistic heat equation, in a simpler form, thus giving a microscopic justification for his phenomenological heat-flux ansatz. He also obtains the small-time asymptotic expansion of the mean square displacement of Brownian motion in static spacetimes. Beyond general relativity, the result could apply to analogue gravity systems. Its link to propulsion is very indirect.
