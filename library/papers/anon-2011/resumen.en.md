The chapter proposes a Holographic Wormhole Drive as a route to near-term superluminal propulsion. It starts from the problem of the Alcubierre metric, which would require negative energy the size of Jupiter. The proposal covers the bubble wall with an array of mini-wormholes and uses an oscillator to adjust the metric functions with little external energy, drawing on vacuum energy. It is a speculative proposal rooted in holographic anthropic cosmology, with no verification shown in the sources.
