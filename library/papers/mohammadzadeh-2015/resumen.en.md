The authors study the quantum entanglement generated by particle pair production in an expanding Friedmann-Robertson-Walker universe. They consider spin 0, 1/2, 1 and 3/2 modes, using the Duffin-Kemmer-Petiau and Rarita-Schwinger equations. They conclude that the absolute value of the spin plays no role and that differences in the behaviour of the entanglement are due to the particles being bosons or fermions.
