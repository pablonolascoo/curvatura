The paper claims to address the physics of unidentified flying objects through antigravity, electrohydrodynamics, electrogravitics and semi-quantum kinetics. It raises questions about how such craft might travel long distances without fuel, harvest energy from nature, or exceed the speed of light. It argues that this science is classified and was obtained by reverse engineering crashed craft. The sources give no verifiable results, so it should be read as a speculative and unvalidated proposal.
