The work starts from the fact that general relativity with only the observed baryons underpredicts the dynamics of galaxies and clusters at accelerations near c times the Hubble constant. It develops an emergent scalar field framework with one hypothesis: ordinary matter continuously emits scalar waves, not gravitational ones, with random phases. The stationary part reproduces Newtonian attraction, and waves from all matter within the causal horizon form a background that sets an acceleration scale of about 7.9 times 10 to the minus 11 m/s². Interference with that background adds an attractive acceleration that mimics dark matter phenomenology.
