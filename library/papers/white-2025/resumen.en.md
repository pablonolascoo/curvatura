The work presents a new class of interior-flat warp bubbles segmented into Gaussian cylinders, called nacelles, as an alternative to the continuous toroidal energy distribution of the Alcubierre model. Using the ADM 3+1 formalism they derive the extrinsic curvature, York time, momentum and energy densities for n = 2, 3 and 4 cylinders. The interior-flat condition keeps internal observers synchronised with external clocks, giving a flat habitable region. The exotic energy is localised in discrete cylindrical channels rather than a diffuse ring.
