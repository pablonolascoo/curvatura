This is a popular guide to rocket science that requires no prior knowledge. It covers rocket dynamics, orbital mechanics, Kessler syndrome and the Apollo and rover landings. The authors argue that rockets are not enough to reach the stars and go on to discuss electrohydrodynamics and anti-gravity, with engines and experiments attributed to Tesla and Thomas Townsend Brown. The text includes speculative claims about UFOs that are not backed by evidence, so it should be read with caution.
