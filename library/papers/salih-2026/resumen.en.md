The authors present the Field of Duality, a phenomenological dual-quaternion framework for coupled wave systems with an intrinsic length scale. From a compact algebraic constraint they derive coupled scalar-vector wave equations with two free parameters, a coupling constant and a duality length. The dispersion relation matches analytically the Bogoliubov spectrum of a weakly interacting Bose-Einstein condensate when the length is identified with the healing length. The form of the dispersion is fixed by the algebraic structure, with no higher-order terms. They extend the framework to sonic horizons and obtain thermal corrections to analogue Hawking radiation.
