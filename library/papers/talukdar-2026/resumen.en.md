The authors connect integrable soliton dynamics with analogue black hole models. Using the AKNS formalism they build an effective metric associated with the complex modified KdV equation and embed its bright one-soliton solution. A horizon structure appears where the temporal component of the metric vanishes. They analyze the scalar invariants and derive the surface gravity and an explicit expression for the analogue Hawking temperature. The horizon and radiation depend on the soliton's amplitude and velocity, so the soliton can be interpreted as a black hole analogue.
