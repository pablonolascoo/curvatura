This thesis explores guided elastic waves in soft materials, mainly elastomers such as Ecoflex that mimic human tissue, and the effect of pre-strain on them. It computes wave speeds and dispersion diagrams in soft plates and ribbons, using a semi-analytical method that matches theory to experiments, and combines acoustoelasticity with viscoelasticity. It applies the work to shear-wave elastography and to the basilar membrane of the cochlea, to explain frequency discrimination. In the last chapter it creates a space-time interface by applying axial stress that grows in time.
