The preprint proposes a model of the quantum vacuum as a relativistic viscous fluid with a fundamental cutoff at the Planck pressure. The viscosity depends on scale, modifies field propagation and introduces dissipative dynamics. From an effective field theory perspective it builds an action with fluid degrees of freedom, quantum corrections and an emergent gravitational term analogous to Sakharov's induced gravity. Observable consequences are derived, such as photon attenuation, energy dependent propagation and corrections to the Unruh and Casimir effects. It also explores the hypothesis that the speed of light is a limit due to a Stokes like drag, which reintroduces a modern ether.
