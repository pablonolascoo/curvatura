The study investigates the stability of thin-shell wormholes in anti-de Sitter space, built from the Hayward-Letelier black hole. It finds that stability depends strongly on the equation of state of the matter. The basic constraints of a traversable wormhole are met, with significant violations of the null and weak energy conditions, indicating exotic matter. Using barotropic, phantom-like and variable Chaplygin equations of state, it sees that barotropic ones remain stable until an event horizon forms and that the variable Chaplygin one gives more stable configurations at high constant values.
