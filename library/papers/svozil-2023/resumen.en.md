This chapter conjectures that in 1948, after Project Sign in the United States, a secret programme was started on advanced topics such as anti-gravity and spaceship development. According to the text, Projects Grudge and Blue Book were promoted publicly to downplay those findings and mislead the public. It portrays people such as Ruppelt, Condon and Hynek as unknowing participants in a government-led staging to deny these technologies and the extraterrestrial origin of a tiny fraction of sightings. It is a conjecture with no evidence in the sources, not a physics result.
