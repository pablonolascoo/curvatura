The author questions whether spacetime geometry must be quantized, comparing that idea to quantizing pressure or temperature. He proposes Chronon Field Theory, in which a smooth timelike covector obeys a variational principle called the Temporal Coherence Principle. In long-wavelength, low-vorticity regimes the dynamics yield an emergent Lorentzian metric and reproduce Einstein's equations to leading order, and the eikonal limit gives Hamilton-Jacobi and Schrodinger-type dynamics. Operational Lorentz invariance is preserved, but small dispersive corrections are predicted, constrained by fast radio burst and multi-messenger observations. It is a theoretical emergent gravity proposal.
