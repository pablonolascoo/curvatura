The study analyzes the quantum dynamics of an electron crossing a black hole horizon. Relying on the equivalence principle, it proposes emulating this with a suitably chirped free-electron wave packet, so that the dynamics is encoded in the packet's interference fringes. The authors derive the electron's explicit wave function at the horizon and give quantitative predictions for its quantum properties. It opens a route to laboratory cosmology with engineered wave packets, within analogue gravity.
