This is the first of three papers presenting the elementary theory and design of a new craft, the sonic-electromagnetic-gravitational spacecraft, which according to the authors does not rely on momentum conservation for propulsion. It rests on four concepts, including a theory of sonics, or manipulation of energy in wave form, radiant hulls that integrate the hull into the propulsion system and the use of high-intensity artificial gravity. It is a conceptual, speculative proposal; the sources show no experimental verification.
