This is a personal exploration repository on whether the exotic matter requirement of the Alcubierre metric can be reinterpreted as a boundary effect, analogous to Casimir, or avoided with shells of classical anisotropic matter. It archives SymPy and NumPy notebooks, parameter sweeps, independent numerical verification with MATLAB and Warp Factory, and evaluations of cited papers. Each result is given with the slice of parameter space it depends on. The provisional conclusion is that within the static slice plus six relaxations explored, no useful classical warp drive exists, without closing the broader question.
