The article criticizes time machines based on general relativity, which change position in spacetime but not entropy, so the traveller keeps ageing, and which would need Jupiter-scale negative energy densities. Instead, using the Generalized Gauge Equation and Einstein-Cartan torsion gravity, it proposes locally reversing the entropy arrow through the superposition of eight vortex optical solitons. It derives torsion-electromagnetism coupling equations and a model for reversing mitochondrial oxidative damage.
