The paper introduces, in flat spacetime, a family of field modes called kappa modes, built as kappa-dependent linear combinations of Minkowski plane waves. They define a family of vacua that interpolates between different quantizations and reduces to the Minkowski vacuum as kappa goes to zero. Each vacuum is shown to be a continuous-mode squeezed vacuum, with a frequency-dependent squeezing parameter satisfying tanh r = exp(-pi nu / kappa). Universal Bogoliubov transformations are also derived, recovering the Minkowski, Rindler and Unruh quantizations as limiting cases. It provides tools relevant to the Unruh effect and vacuum energy.
