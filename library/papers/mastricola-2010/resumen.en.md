This thesis aims to show that relativistic perturbations should be treated like the usual perturbations in planet-satellite systems, since their specific forces are comparable. It gains interest from advances in propulsion such as light sails, continuous propulsion or artificial neutron stars, which could open interstellar travel. The perturbations were simulated on a general orbit and a Molniya-type orbit using Matlab's ODE45 integrator. The results indicate that relativistic specific forces produce variations in the classical orbital elements, with magnitudes and effects similar to conventional perturbations.
