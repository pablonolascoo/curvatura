The article compares cosmographic energy conditions in f(R,T) gravity, an alternative model proposed in response to the standard cosmological model's difficulties with accelerated expansion. It checks whether the results change depending on whether the cosmographic parameters (q0, j0, s0) are obtained with the usual Taylor expansion or with Pade and Chebyshev rational approximations. It thus shows how cosmographic energy conditions can serve for model selection. It is relevant because energy conditions are what warp and wormhole solutions violate.
