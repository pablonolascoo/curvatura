The article, written in French, analyses military space foresight, that is, writing about space warfare, especially work that comes from the margins, such as science fiction prototyping and design fiction. It seeks to understand their forms, authors, themes and writing methods, which mix science, geopolitics and fiction. It draws on sources published in Europe and the United States, including the three volumes of the French Red Team, to show the particularities and limits of this effort to think about future war in space. It is a study in history and social science, not physics.
