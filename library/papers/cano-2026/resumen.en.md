The study examines the effective potential, spatial curvature and refractive lensing produced by a configuration called the antisymmetric dark state, introduced in earlier work. Inspired by analogue gravity and Bose-Einstein condensate experiments, it sets up a purely spatial quantum framework in which geometry emerges from the coherence of the wavefunction, through a single radial metric factor. The effective potential is regular and confining, allowing bound orbits even at zero angular momentum. The Ricci scalar is finite and tends to zero far from the centre. The metric factor is also mapped to a refractive index to analyse lensing.
