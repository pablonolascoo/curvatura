This text is a conditional theoretical and engineering sketch of a "holographic AU drive" for inertialess interstellar travel. The author states that everything depends on his framework (Acta Universi) being confirmed experimentally and on quantum, topological and bio-informational technologies that do not yet exist. The ship schemes, safety protocols, jump-time estimates and the 2025-2045 roadmap are therefore to be read as a thought experiment, not as a design or a promise of technology.
