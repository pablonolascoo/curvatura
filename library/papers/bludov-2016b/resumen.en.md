The authors show that a vector matter-wave soliton in a Bose-Einstein condensate loaded in an optical lattice can escape from a parabolic trap in a way resembling Hawking emission. The particle-antiparticle pair is emulated by a low-amplitude bright-bright soliton in a two-component condensate with effective masses of opposite sign. The parabolic potential spatially separates the components: one oscillates periodically while the negative-mass one escapes the trap. The mechanism of atom transfer between components is also discussed.
