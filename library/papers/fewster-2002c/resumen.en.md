Quantum fields violate the weak energy condition: the renormalised energy density has no lower bound across states. For scalar and electromagnetic fields it is known that weighted averages along timelike curves do have lower bounds, the so-called quantum weak energy inequalities. This paper establishes them for Dirac and Majorana fields with mass greater than or equal to zero on four-dimensional globally hyperbolic spacetimes, averaging along arbitrary timelike curves. The proof relies on the microlocal characterisation of Hadamard states. It is key to bounding negative energy in wormholes and warp drives.
