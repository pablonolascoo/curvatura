The authors ask whether two classical time-machine geometries, Ori's compact vacuum-core metric and a four-dimensional generalization of Misner space, remain exact solutions in a modified gravity theory that couples curvature, the matter Lagrangian and a scalar field with its kinetic term. Using an explicit model and a harmonic scalar profile, they compute the curvature invariants, the modified field equations and the effective stress-energy components in both backgrounds. It is a geometric analysis of closed timelike curves in modified theories, related to the energy conditions such solutions demand.
