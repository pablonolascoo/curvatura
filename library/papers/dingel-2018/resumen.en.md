The authors establish an optical analogy between the Thomas rotation of special relativity and the phase response of an all-pass filter built from a microring resonator in an integrated photonic circuit. They relate the filter's coupling coefficient to the product of the normalized relativistic velocities, and the single-pass phase shift to the generating angle of the two velocities. They add a circuit that converts this phase-encoded angle into a measurable intensity. This opens the possibility of studying special relativity phenomena in a lab-on-a-chip setting.
