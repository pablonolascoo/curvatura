The work studies the quantum Dirac field in flat (n+1)-dimensional spacetime and derives a lower bound, in the form of a quantum inequality, on the energy density averaged with spacetime sampling functions. The inequality is state-independent, resembles the temporal quantum energy inequality, and is stronger for the massive field than for the massless one. It also gives the concrete results in two and four dimensions. It limits how much negative energy can appear in fermionic fields.
