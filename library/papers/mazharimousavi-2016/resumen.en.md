For a generic f(R) admitting a polynomial expansion, the authors find the near-throat wormhole solution. They derive necessary conditions for wormholes to exist in these theories, for both vanishing and nonvanishing matter sources. With no external sources, the energy conditions are violated. With a particular choice of energy-momentum tensor, the geometry satisfies the weak energy condition, and even the strong one for a range of parameters. It is relevant because it shows when modified gravity can avoid exotic matter in wormholes.
