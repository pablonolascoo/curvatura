The paper is a case study of explanatory and illustrative visualisations of special and general relativity, cosmology and astrophysics, produced for the Einstein Year 2005. It discusses strategies based on physics didactics and on media types such as magazines, television, talks and museums. It contributes technical improvements, including GPU special-relativistic rendering, relativistic ray tracing on manifolds with several charts, and interactive visualisation of gravitational light deflection. It is science communication and computer graphics.
