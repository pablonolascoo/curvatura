The work explores the possible existence of charged traversable wormholes with a pseudo-isothermal profile in a modified gravity theory with minimal matter-geometry coupling. They use Morris-Thorne geometry and anisotropic equations, with two types of redshift function, constant and variable, giving two shape functions that meet the criteria and are asymptotically flat. They analyse the null energy condition to see whether exotic fluid is needed, the active gravitational mass and the complexity factor, which is minimal near the throat. They conclude that the models are viable.
