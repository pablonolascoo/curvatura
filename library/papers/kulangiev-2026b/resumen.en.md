The work derives the Painlevé-Gullstrand flow as a collective mean-field effect of many vortex-Gausson solitons, the topological defects of a logarithmic condensate acting as a superfluid vacuum. Self-consistency selects that flow uniquely, while the static solution fails. Assuming each soliton sources the acoustic metric, they obtain an effective gravitational constant that depends on the healing length and the vacuum density. Matching it to Newton's constant puts the healing length at the order of the Planck length. It is an emergent gravity model.
