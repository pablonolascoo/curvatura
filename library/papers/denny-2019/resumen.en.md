This book chapter discusses the cost of reaching space and the value of sending people there. It then reviews propulsion systems that are still rockets but not chemical ones: some already in use, some that will exist one day and some that probably never will, yet are still studied in case they lead to the stars. It examines what interstellar travel would require and presents a propulsion concept that might achieve it without rockets. It closes with a tour of more speculative ideas for space travel after rockets.
