Starting from the tension between quantum nonlocality and relativistic causality, the article explores scenarios in which Lorentz invariance is emergent and light might depend weakly on the coherence of nearby quantum systems. It introduces a phenomenological model with that coupling and proposes a quantum-controlled Mach-Zehnder interferometer comparing coherent and incoherent ancillary states with the same populations. Standard quantum mechanics predicts identical statistics, whereas the model predicts excess phase noise. The sensitivity is derived and illustrative experimental bounds on the coupling parameter are estimated for fibre interferometers.
