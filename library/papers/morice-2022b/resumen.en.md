This review report describes a study of wave packet dynamics and eigenstate localisation in generalised lattice models whose low-energy dynamics mimics a quantum field theory in 1+1 curved spacetime, aiming to build black hole analogues. Zero-energy packets slow down critically, a sign of a horizon, while non-zero-energy ones bounce back before reaching it. The eigenstates localise as power laws, and the result matches the geodesics of the effective metric.
