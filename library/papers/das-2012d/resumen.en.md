This textbook chapter introduces tensor analysis on differentiable manifolds, the mathematical basis of general relativity. It begins by defining an N-dimensional differentiable manifold and sets out the assumptions of the definition: a topology, the Hausdorff property and paracompactness, which is equivalent to having a countable basis of open sets. It is background mathematics with no physical results of its own, useful for formulating the metrics and curvature used in studies of warp drives and wormholes.
