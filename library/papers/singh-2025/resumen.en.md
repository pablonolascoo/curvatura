The work proposes changing how spaceflight is done: instead of applying an external force, engineer the geometry of spacetime, or field analogues, so that desired trajectories emerge as geodesics. It presents NEXUS, a symbolic-discovery engine that derives equations using variational calculus, symmetry constraints and geometric priors. With it, it obtains new classes of metrics and field configurations for directed motion without propulsion. Numerical simulations show spiral, slingshot and drift-corridor trajectories with no energy input, conserving relativistic invariants.
