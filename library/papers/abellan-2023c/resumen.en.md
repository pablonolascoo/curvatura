This work rewrites the Alcubierre warp metric with spherical symmetry and solves the Einstein equations for two kinds of matter: a perfect fluid and an anisotropic fluid with a cosmological constant. The authors examine the energy conditions in both cases and find enough freedom for them to be satisfied. When the bubble is time independent, the metric has a timelike Killing vector and every energy condition holds except the strong one. A barotropic equation of state familiar from cosmology also emerges naturally in that case. It matters for warp drives because it points to matter configurations that are more realistic than the usual ones.
