This textbook covers semiclassical and stochastic gravity. It starts from the fact that quantum field theory in curved spacetime is needed to understand processes in the early universe and black holes, such as the Hawking effect. It examines the back-reaction of quantum fields on the background, which matters near the Planck time, through the semiclassical Einstein equation, with inflationary cosmology as an example, and the quantum field fluctuations that underlie stochastic gravity and describe metric fluctuations or spacetime foam. It summarizes more than four decades of development and underlies semiclassical treatments involving negative energy.
