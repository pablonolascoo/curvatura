The authors study how small-amplitude localized oscillating structures form in Bose-Einstein condensates trapped in two- and three-dimensional optical lattices. They use regions with effective masses of opposite sign and the kp method to map the Gross-Pitaevskii equation onto nonlinear Klein-Gordon or Ginzburg-Landau-Higgs equations. They predict atomic oscillons and kink-shaped states, with a critical particle number needed to form them in finite lattices. This opens the way to simulating cosmological processes and hyperbolic metamaterials with cold atoms.
