The work shows that when a warp bubble moves faster than light, Doppler shifted photons of growing energy and event horizons appear on its leading and trailing walls, and that this arises from shift-only solutions derived from the geometrical interpretation of general relativity. It argues that these phenomena do not appear in a properly formulated polarizable vacuum warp solution if the refractive index inside the bubble is precisely controlled. The formalism fits the analogue gravity of the polarizable vacuum model, a variable speed of light reading of general relativity.
