The authors present a spherically symmetric wormhole in Einstein gravity coupled to a phantom scalar field and a nonlinear electromagnetic field. Numerical results show that the solution violates the null energy condition and that, as the parameters change, the total ADM mass goes from positive to negative. They analyse the light ring and its observational properties. With negative mass the usual ring does not appear, only a special unstable one at the throat, caused by the repulsion of the negative mass on both sides. They also draw the embedding diagram.
