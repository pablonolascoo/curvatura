The authors show a new class of higher-derivative interaction terms that can be added to any low-derivative real scalar, so that first-order perturbations on that background are ghost-free, without imposing extra constraints. They also present related theories with a stabilizer variable and a constraint that are ghost-free without restricting to a perturbative expansion. In these, the constrained second-order self-interaction does not necessarily alter luminal propagation, avoiding the superluminality problems of some dark energy and inflation models with first-order interactions. It is a field theory work, not a propulsion one.
