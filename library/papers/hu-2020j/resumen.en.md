This chapter is a pedagogical guide to the infrared behaviour of interacting quantum fields in de Sitter space, a topic active since the 1980s. It organizes three lines of work, namely Euclidean zero-mode dominance, Lorentzian interacting quantum field theory and classical stochastic field theory, and explains the physics and technique of each. Tools include the 2PI effective action, the effective infrared dimension, Langevin and Fokker-Planck equations and the nonperturbative renormalization group. It shows how these routes relate and points to promising recent papers.
