Forward pointed out that a gravitational dipole of ordinary and negative matter would self accelerate, giving the ultimate propellantless propulsion, but negative matter has not been found in nature. Since E = mc squared, the author proposes creating it in the laboratory using negative energies. He presents two routes to stationary, charged negative effective masses: one based on Weber's electrodynamics, with electrons inside a highly charged dielectric sphere, and another using asymmetric charge distributions achievable with electrets. With suitable geometry and charge densities, he derives negative mass regimes that would give negative energies many orders of magnitude larger than those from the Casimir effect.
