The work seeks a model of matter that accounts for electro-gravity. It measures how far a test particle departs from a geodesic through its non-geodesic acceleration and, to avoid reliance on specific trajectories, introduces a scalar curvature field based on a new notion of time. That time is the maximal measurable proper time from each event back to the initial singularity, not locally observable, though its gradient is local. If several curves cross the same event, the gradient is not parallel to itself, which defines a curvature field. This leads to a formalism that would replace the conventional stress-energy-momentum tensor, for classical and quantum physics, and to a theory of electro-gravity.
