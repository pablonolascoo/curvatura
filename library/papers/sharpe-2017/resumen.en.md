The article presents a unifying model, obtained through observation, citation and deduction, covering dark matter, dark energy, gravity, space and negative energy. According to the author, it would give a fundamental definition of space and gravity, support dark energy theory, explain dark matter, reveal additional structure in baryonic atoms, shed light on baryogenesis and revise estimates of the universe's longevity. It also touches on antigravity, artificial gravity, gravitational waves, wormholes and interstellar space travel. These are the author's claims, with no verifiable results in the sources.
