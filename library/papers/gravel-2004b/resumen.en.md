This work continues the study of Krasnikov tubes, structures that would allow superluminal travel, for the case of a tube of small mass. It first analyses geodesics directed at the tube and shows that superluminal shifting is a generic property of those aimed at it, not only of those entering through its mouths. It then argues that, if present in sufficient numbers, microtubes could achieve a pre-decoupling homogenization without inflation in an open neighbourhood of our particle horizon.
