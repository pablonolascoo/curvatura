The article argues that colonising the stars is practically impossible under current relativity and that a breakthrough in physics beyond it is needed. Since research over the past century focused on the quantum, it proposes considering classical extensions of relativity that unify general relativity and electrodynamics, so that spacetime and gravity could be controlled with electromagnetic fields. Such extensions would offer a mechanism for electromagnetic distortion of spacetime through a mediating scalar field and a hyperspace dimension. According to the author, the theory has no conflict with experiment, and the nature of charge might provide a testable hypothesis.
