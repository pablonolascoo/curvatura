Alcubierre exhibited a spacetime that, within general relativity, allows superluminal travel if matter with negative energy density can exist, and conjectured that similar techniques could yield closed causal loops and travel into the past. This paper verifies the conjecture with a simple modification of Alcubierre's model that needs no extra assumptions and in which causal loops appear. It also notes that this mechanism differs in essential ways from the one found by Gott using cosmic strings. The result shows that warp drives raise causality problems.
