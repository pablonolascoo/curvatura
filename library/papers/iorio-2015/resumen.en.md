The article argues for building an experimental facility to test fundamental theories using co-responding systems, that is, systems at our energy scale whose behaviour mirrors that of unreachable high-energy systems. It focuses on two scenarios the authors know well: hadron production in high-energy collisions seen as an Unruh phenomenon, and graphene as a quantum field theory in curved spacetime, which they regard as their main bet. It is a programmatic argument about what to do after CERN, with no numerical results.
