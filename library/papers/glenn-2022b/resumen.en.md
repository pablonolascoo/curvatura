The work argues mathematically that it is possible to obtain a positive energy density for warping spacetime in the Alcubierre warp field equations. The key is a complex shaping function whose imaginary part exceeds the real part, which makes the solution for the required energy density positive. It proposes an experiment with a material whose dielectric constant has a larger imaginary than real component, placed in a radio-frequency-driven resonant cavity, which would reproduce the shaping function of the Alcubierre metric. It is a theoretical proposal that sets out a structure for future experiments.
