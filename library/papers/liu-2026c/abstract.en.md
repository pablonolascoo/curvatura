We study complete metrics on area enlargeable manifolds through the spectral constant $Λ_γ(g)$, the infimum of the Rayleigh quotient of $-Δ+γ\mathrm{scal}_g$ over compactly supported functions. We prove that every complete metric on an area enlargeable $m$-manifold, $m\ge2$, satisfies $Λ_γ(g)\le0$ for all $γ>(m-1)/(4m)$. For an area enlargeable manifold $M_1$ and an oriented manifold $M_2$ of the same dimension, we prove that $M_1\#M_2$ is area enlargeable if and only if $M_2$ admits a spin covering. Both summands may be noncompact. Consequently, if the universal covering of $M_2$ is spin, then $M_1\#M_2$ carries neither a complete metric of positive scalar curvature nor a complete metric with positive $γ$-spectral constant for $γ>(m-1)/(4m)$. The threshold $(m-1)/(4m)$ is optimal. At the endpoint, the obstruction holds for metrics whose scalar curvature is bounded from below, but explicit complete warped metrics on $\mathbf{T}^{m-1}\times\mathbf{R}$ show that it fails in general. We also prove that the locally constant and the classical formulations of area enlargeability coincide in dimensions at least two.
