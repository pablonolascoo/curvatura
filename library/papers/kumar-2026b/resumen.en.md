The work presents a framework in which gravitation, inertia and wave dynamics emerge from the response of a vacuum with finite potential capacity, described by a scalar vacuum-potential field whose equilibrium value at infinity is c squared. Static relaxation of localized deficits reproduces Newtonian gravity in the coarse-grained limit, and time-dependent redistribution gives waves governed by a universal equation. Finite capacity implies intrinsic bounds, a maximum force of c to the fourth over G and signal speed at most c, and a dispersion relation with Planck-suppressed corrections consistent with current constraints.
