The work addresses whether a dynamical connection exists between tensor networks and gravity, a question that remains unsolved. A tensor network is known to generate anti-de Sitter geometry through entanglement renormalization. The authors propose combining tensor networks, entanglement entropy, canonical quantization of gravity and the holographic principle. They argue that gravitational dynamics can emerge from a tensor network if its wave function satisfies the Wheeler-DeWitt equation. It is a theoretical proposal for emergent gravity.
