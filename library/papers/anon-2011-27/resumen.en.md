This is a review, written in Chinese, of theoretical progress on the Casimir effect in flat plate structures, with particular detail on the repulsive Casimir force. It explains that the rapid development of metamaterials, which let researchers control electromagnetic properties through artificial microstructures, makes repulsive and restoring forces attainable. The authors point out that these basic results will have broad use in micro and nano electromechanical systems. For negative energy and exotic propulsion it is relevant because the Casimir effect is a known source of negative energy density.
