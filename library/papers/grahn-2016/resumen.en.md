The article starts from reports of thrust without reaction mass in closed metal cavities fed with microwaves, which seem to violate the law of action and reaction because no exhaust is visible. The author suggests the exhaust exists in a form that has been overlooked: inside the cavity photons interfere and some travel in the same direction with opposite phases. At destructive interference the fields cancel, but the photons carry on, do not reflect off the walls and leave the resonator, taking momentum with them. That would give the opposite reaction. Theoretical and practical consequences are discussed.
