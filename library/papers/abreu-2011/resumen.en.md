This thesis studies aspects of general relativity that do not require solving the Einstein equations explicitly. Using the Kodama vector it builds a geometrically preferred coordinate system and generalizes surface gravity in two ways, one for radial null geodesics in spherical symmetry and a vectorial one, which it uses to bound the entropy of static and rotating horizonless objects. It also develops improved versions of the Raychaudhuri equation, including a two-congruence form. Finally it addresses quantum inequalities and the quantum interest conjecture, giving a variational proof of one version of the latter in 3+1 Minkowski space. It is relevant to the limits on negative energy density.
