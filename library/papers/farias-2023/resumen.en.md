The paper uses the energy conditions together with cosmography, a model-independent tool, to constrain f(R,T) gravity models of the form f(R)+f(T), where R is the Ricci scalar and T the trace of the energy-momentum tensor. The motivation is that many alternatives to LambdaCDM give the same results, creating degeneracy among them. With the observed parameter values, the results indicate that quintessence models are preferred as dark energy in this theory. It is cosmology, using the energy conditions as a constraining tool.
