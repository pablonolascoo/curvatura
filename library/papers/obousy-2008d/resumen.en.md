The work starts from extra-dimension models in which the Casimir effect would be a candidate for the cosmological constant. It shows that a very advanced civilization could, in principle, manipulate the radius of the extra dimension to adjust that constant locally. That adjustment would produce expansion or contraction of spacetime around a spacecraft, an exotic field propulsion. Because the expansion of space is not limited by relativity, a faster-than-light warp drive would result. Energy requirements are calculated and an ultimate speed limit set by Planckian bounds on the extra dimensions is found.
