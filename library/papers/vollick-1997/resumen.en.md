The author derives the field equations and energy-momentum tensor of a charged ideal fluid coupled to a scalar field. He shows that an interaction energy-momentum tensor exists that can give negative energy densities even though the fluid and fields have positive energy densities. He computes the energy density and mass of a fluid sphere in the weak-field limit and finds they can be negative under certain conditions. With negative mass, the entire interior is exotic matter, that is, it violates the weak energy condition.
