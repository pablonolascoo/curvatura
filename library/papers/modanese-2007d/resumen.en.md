In the functional integral of quantum gravity one usually works in the stationary phase approximation around the saddle point where spacetime is flat. The authors show that a level line in functional space departs from that point, containing a family of static non-flat metrics with zero scalar curvature, depending on a continuous mass parameter. Each can be perturbed by an arbitrary function while still satisfying the zero-action condition. They thus find zero modes of the action with non-vanishing measure, which would contribute as vacuum fluctuations. The title points to large virtual masses in the vacuum.
