The article studies the Hamiltonian dynamics of a spacecraft on the backgrounds of the Alcubierre and Gödel metrics. The authors obtain the Hamiltonian vector fields governing the system's evolution, construct and discuss recursion operators that generate constants of motion, and describe the corresponding master symmetries. It is a mathematical work on integrability in warp drive metrics and in universes with closed timelike curves, providing tools to analyze trajectories in those geometries.
