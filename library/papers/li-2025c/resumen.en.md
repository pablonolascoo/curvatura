The paper asks how Lorentzian causal structure can emerge from a pregeometric substrate. For a well defined class of ferromagnetically coupled "chronon" models with quartic norm pinning, it proves, with strictly positive Gibbs probability, a percolating domain where the field is smooth, future directed, unit norm timelike and twist free. Under four operational axioms it proves that no other signature can sustain such behavior, so the Lorentzian phase is exclusive. In addition, measurement acts as a boundary induced selector of this phase, with exponential convergence. It is a mathematical work on the origin of causality.
