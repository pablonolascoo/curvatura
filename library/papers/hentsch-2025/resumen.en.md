The work introduces a metric-free model of null dynamics in 3+1 dimensions, based on a single algebraic constraint that defines a null cone. Within it they identify a universal critical shear value marking the transition from divergence to refocusing of null trajectories. It arises from the algebraic interplay of expansion and twist and holds across three geometric embeddings. It also governs a bifurcation in the Jacobi equation and matches a phase transition in a PT-symmetric quantum analogue.
