This work gives an initial formulation of how negative energies might be produced at the Planck scale and what their consequences would be. In quantum field theory, negative-energy solutions are attributed to loops and vacuum bubbles and regarded as a by-product of virtual particles with no effect in ordinary spacetime. The author instead postulates that such virtual particles could produce real physical effects at the Planck scale. The available summary gives no numbers or specific methods. Its relevance to exotic propulsion is as a possible microscopic source of negative energy.
