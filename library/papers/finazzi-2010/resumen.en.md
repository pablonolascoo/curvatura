The authors analyze whether superluminal warp bubbles would survive quantum effects, even if the required exotic matter were available. They carry out an exact numerical calculation of the renormalized stress-energy tensor across the whole bubble, extending earlier work that looked only at the wall and the centre. They find it grows exponentially in time near the front wall, strongly supporting that these geometries are unstable against semiclassical back-reaction. The result appears to implement the chronology protection conjecture.
