This book chapter surveys deep space missions: the origin and types of stars, supernovae, black holes, dark matter and dark energy, and the space radiation that affects crews. It reviews trajectories and propulsion based on current science, such as antimatter, fission, fusion and the interstellar ramjet. It ends with theoretical options such as warping or folding spacetime and wormholes, and with countermeasures against radiation. It provides context for the propulsion requirements of interstellar travel.
