This work proposes a warp drive concept based on the fractal potential of the so-called McGinty equation, as a route to faster-than-light travel. After explaining the Alcubierre metric and its limitations, it sets out a methodology of AI-assisted mathematical analysis and numerical simulation, an energy assessment and a comparison with the Alcubierre framework. The contribution is a conceptual proposal; the available text shows no verifiable quantitative results. Its interest lies in looking for alternatives to the exotic matter needed by the Alcubierre bubble.
