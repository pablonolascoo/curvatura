The preprint argues that the accepted mathematical interpretation of the Minkowski and FLRW metrics is incorrect, and that this has prevented a conclusion on the true shape and size of the universe. According to the author, with the corrected interpretation either metric works, and the universe would be the three-dimensional hypersurface of a four-dimensional hypersphere, finite and not flat. It thus challenges the measurements that give a flat universe. It is a heterodox cosmological proposal with no direct link to propulsion.
