The team uses Belousov-Zhabotinsky reaction-diffusion chemistry to reproduce gravitational lensing in a table-top experiment. They observe chemical waves crossing non-planar, quasi-two-dimensional moulds and reproduce the waveforms in simulations using planar waves with variable diffusion. They tune that diffusion to match the speed of light in Schwarzschild coordinates near a spherical mass, so the propagation approximates Einstein's light deflection. They also discuss varying diffusion or reaction rates with gels, illumination, electric fields or temperature gradients. It is an analogue-gravity demonstration.
