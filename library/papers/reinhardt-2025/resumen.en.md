This work proposes "artificial spacetimes" as a geometric method for controlling resource-limited robots, such as microrobots without onboard computation. It shows that reactive robots following control fields obey the same dynamics as light rays in general relativity, allowing techniques from relativity and optics to be used to build and analyze those fields. The fields guide robots through structured environments, avoiding boundaries and completing tasks, even when static. It is validated experimentally with silicon-based microrobots.
