The paper shows how computers help visualize relativity. It presents new results from the authors' own work: (2+1)-dimensional Minkowski diagrams with two spatial dimensions, an extended wormhole visualization, and the illustration of accretion discs approximated by a rigidly rotating disc of dust. It also discusses earlier work from the group, such as interactive and immersive visualization and the visualization of the warp drive metric. It helps build intuition for geometries such as the warp drive.
