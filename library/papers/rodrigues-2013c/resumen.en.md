This work, written in Portuguese, starts from the view that the constancy of the speed of light seems a barrier for interstellar journeys, since infinite energy would be needed to reach it. In general relativity, however, arbitrarily higher global speeds can be obtained without violating that local constancy. The so called warp drive metrics are a theoretical means to do this, and Alcubierre's is the case that inspired the term. The author reviews special and general relativity and some works on solutions that allow superluminal speeds.
