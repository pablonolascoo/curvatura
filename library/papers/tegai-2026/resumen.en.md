The study examines whether Alcubierre's warp drive allows superluminal motion and shows that the conclusion depends on the convention chosen for simultaneity and distance measurement. With the radar convention, the average speed of the journey does not exceed the speed of light. However, within the Fermi coordinate framework faster-than-light travel remains possible. For certain parameter values, superluminal motion is seen in both the ship's frame and the destination port's frame.
