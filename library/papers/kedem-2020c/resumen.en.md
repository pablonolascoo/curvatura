Electrons in type II Weyl semimetals show one way propagation, which produces total reflection at an endpoint, as with black hole horizons seen from the inside. Junctions between type I and type II materials lead to equations identical to those near a horizon, but the authors suggest the physical implications are quite different from what is conventionally expected in that setting. The time reversed configuration, a white hole, would also be physically accessible in these materials. It is a condensed matter analogue of horizon physics.
