The authors introduce analogue black holes based on the ideal magnetohydrodynamic equations. As acoustic black holes trap phonons at the sonic horizon and emit Hawking radiation, in these ones magnetoacoustic and Alfven waves would be trapped and emit quantized vibrations they call magnephonons and alphonons. They propose creating them in the laboratory with a variable-cross-section tube in a uniform magnetic field and a super-magnetoacoustic or super-Alfvenic flow. The Hawking temperature depends on the magnetic field, the fluid number density and the tube radius, and for a typical setup they estimate about 0.0266 K.
