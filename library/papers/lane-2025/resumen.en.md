The article argues from epistemology that the extraterrestrial hypothesis, that an alien civilization is active on Earth today, should not be an academic taboo. It holds that advances in biology make it improbable that complex life is rare in the galaxy and that the usual objections ignore possible motives and means. It argues that rational goals such as self-preservation would lead to gathering information and concealment. It concludes that the hypothesis overturns no well-tested science. It is a philosophical text with no physical propulsion proposal.
