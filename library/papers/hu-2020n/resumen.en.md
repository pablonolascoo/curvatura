The chapter reviews work on regularising the stress-energy tensor of quantum fields in Schwarzschild spacetime and on information loss, complementarity and the firewall conjecture. It then treats two problems: the back-reaction of Hawking radiation on the black hole in the quasi-stationary regime, solved in the far field with the influence functional, showing nonlocal dissipation and coloured noise, and the metric fluctuations of the horizon of an evaporating black hole. It concludes, like Bekenstein, that accumulated fluctuations become significant well before the Planckian regime.
