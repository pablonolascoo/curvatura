The authors present an axially symmetric pure-radiation spacetime that admits circular closed timelike curves in a plane of constant z. It is regular everywhere, with no curvature singularities, and locally isometric to non-vacuum pp-wave spacetimes. Computing the Lyapunov exponent of the deviation vector, they find these curves are stable under linear perturbations. Non-circular curves outside the plane also exist. A modification of the metric produces a region that behaves like a time machine.
