Quantum field theory allows negative local energy densities, but restricts them through quantum energy inequalities, which average the density over time, space or both. The authors treat the free Rarita-Schwinger field and obtain a bound on the density averaged over space and time. That bound coincides with the known temporal inequality and turns out weaker than those for scalar and Dirac fields. This supports the conjecture that the more freedom a field has, the more easily it displays negative energy.
