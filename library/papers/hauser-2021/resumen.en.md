The last of a series of three articles, it tries to clarify the physics behind contradictory experiments on the neutron lifetime and the discrepancy between particle physics predictions and experiments on dark matter and dark energy. To this end it introduces the concepts of negative and hypercomplex matter, replacing real numbers with hypercomplex numbers, in place of extra spatial dimensions and supersymmetry, which require new particles and internal symmetries. The title announces hypercomplex gravity-like fields and propellantless space propulsion. It is a speculative theoretical proposal.
