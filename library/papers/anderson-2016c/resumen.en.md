The authors simulate the Alcubierre spacetime with a Tamm medium that is asymptotically identical to vacuum and has continuous parameters, so that it could in principle be built as a nanostructured metamaterial. They characterize ray trajectories in the geometric-optics regime and find evanescent propagation directions inside the region corresponding to the bubble. Trajectories depend strongly on the bubble's speed and direction, and much less on the thickness of its transition zone. For rays moving along with the bubble, it acts as a focusing lens, especially at high speed.
