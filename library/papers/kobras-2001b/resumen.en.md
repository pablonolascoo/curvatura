The article extends image-based rendering, a computer graphics technique for photorealistic images, to the visualization of general relativistic effects in a restricted class of spacetimes. The authors propose a generalized aberration formula that treats special and general relativistic effects on the same footing, so that general relativistic rendering can be seen as an extension of special relativistic rendering. As an example, they show snapshots from the viewpoint of an observer traveling at warp speed. It is useful for visualizing how a warp drive trip would look.
