The authors formulate a new class of traversable wormhole metrics. They start with a metric whose temporal component is exponential in r and whose spatial part is fixed, then swap the roles, and finally generalise both components with exponential functions. They study properties such as throat radius, stability, energy conditions, singularities, effective refractive index, innermost stable circular orbit, photon sphere, quasinormal modes and gravitational entropy. The throat radii are consistent and free of singularities, and all of them violate the null energy condition near the throat.
