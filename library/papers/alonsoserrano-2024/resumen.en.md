The paper starts from the fact that any discussion of time travel runs into logical contradictions, and that appealing to quantum gravity is not enough because it carries the problem of time. As a way out, it proposes using the Page-Wootters formalism, with its gauge-theoretic reinterpretation, in which time is an emergent notion. The authors open a program of studying toy models that implement the Hamiltonian constraint in quantum theory, to see what an emergent time implies for the possibility or impossibility of time travel. It is a conceptual work related to closed timelike curves.
