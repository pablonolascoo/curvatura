This chapter proposes stochastic gravity as an alternative framework for studying the generation of primordial inhomogeneities in inflationary models, able to include effects beyond linear perturbations of the inflaton. It shows that the correlation functions from the Einstein-Langevin equation coincide with those of the usual quantization when the perturbations are linear. It illustrates this with a simple chaotic inflation model on a quasi de Sitter universe and proves the equivalence of stochastic and quantum correlations at linear order.
