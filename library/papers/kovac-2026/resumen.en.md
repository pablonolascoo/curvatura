The paper puts forward an alternative mathematical hypothesis: particle properties would be three-dimensional projections of complex topological defects in a four-dimensional manifold, rather than fundamental points. It applies the geometry of kaleidoscopic iterated function systems to a zero-viscosity hydrodynamic framework and points to a formal isomorphism between quantum numbers and 4D fractal attractors. It does not aim to replace known interactions but to offer a geometric lens in which mass, spin and localization emerge from higher-dimensional fluid mechanics.
