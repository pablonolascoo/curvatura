This book chapter constructs the closed time path (CTP) two particle irreducible (2PI) effective action to two loop order. The CTP formalism gives real and causal equations of motion, and the 2PI formalism lets one treat critical phenomena. The large N expansion yields nonperturbative evolution equations with a covariantly conserved stress-energy tensor. At leading order the matter effective action gives semiclassical gravity and at next order stochastic gravity. It treats O(N) Phi-4 fields and a Yukawa coupling between scalars and spinors as an example of fermions in curved spacetime.
