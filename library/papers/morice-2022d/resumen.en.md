This peer review report describes a study of wave packet dynamics and eigenstate localization in generalized lattice models whose low-energy physics mimics a quantum field theory in (1+1)D curved spacetime, aiming to create black hole analogues. In tight-binding models with power-law hopping there is a critical slowdown of zero-energy wave packets, a sign of a horizon, while packets with non-zero energy bounce back before reaching it. A semiclassical description matches the geodesics of the effective metric.
