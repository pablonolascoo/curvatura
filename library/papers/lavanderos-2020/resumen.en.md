This management and organizational design article proposes a new approach to the concept of viability. It argues for moving from the concept of object to that of relation: a viable unit cannot be separated from its surroundings, and what is external is not a separate entity. Within that framework, value creation relies mainly on intangibles, and its propagation requires a relational field. Emergent design, which it calls a warp network, would be a relational process built on co-autonomy and a heterarchical operating structure. It belongs to organization theory and has no relation to physical propulsion.
