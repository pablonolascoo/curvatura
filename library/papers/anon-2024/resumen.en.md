The article shows how formulas of the Gauss-Bonnet-Chern type, native to Riemannian geometry, can be applied in general relativity. The aim is to estimate the energy density needed to change the topology of spacetime. It relies on the link between curvature and topology and on the role of topological defects. It is relevant to wormholes and other structures that require altering topology, since it offers a way to bound their energy cost.
