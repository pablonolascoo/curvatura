The author starts from a generalized gauge transformation theory meant to unify the four interactions and studies how to convert the electromagnetic tensor into the Weyl tensor for curvature-driven propulsion. He derives the mathematical form of that transformation and analyzes its use in spacecraft with dynamic curvature engines, wormhole exploration and saucer-shaped engines. He compares the energy needs of negative-energy models with those of an electromagnetic model and concludes the latter would be more advantageous for superluminal travel. It is a theoretical proposal without experimental verification.
