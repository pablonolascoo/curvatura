We prove here several tightness results for such quantum inequalities as 1) the comparison of operator norm and product norm of $d$-local hamiltonians, 2) Bohnenblust--Hille inequality for $d$-local hamiltonians, 3) for quantum Fourier entropy-influence conjecture. We give a deterministic prove of the sharpness of Bohnenblust--Hille exponent. We also discuss the quantum Aaronson--Ambainis conjecture in a special case of anti-commuting Pauli strings.
