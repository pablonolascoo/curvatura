This is a referee report on a paper proposing an experimental realization of the Unruh effect with interacting ultracold fermions in optical lattices. The proposal uses a sudden quench equivalent to accelerating the vacuum, with varying interaction strength, on a real temperature background. An inversion of statistics is seen in the low-lying excitations of the Wightman function, from the competition between the spacetime and BCS Bogoliubov transformations. It opens perspectives for quantum gravity simulators, and treats the pairing function on cylinder and torus geometries.
