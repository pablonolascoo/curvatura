The paper builds an optical analogy of lightlike geodesics in spacetimes with frame dragging, in particular Kerr. It equates parts of the deflection angle expression of the geodesics with that of an optical medium with a gradient refractive index. The resulting conditions separate the index from the frame-dragging effect and give an oblate spheroidally symmetric index and a new optical Lagrangian, placed in a non-Euclidean background space with purely spatial frame dragging. The author suggests the analogy could generalize the notion of analogue magnetism and help the study of gravitational lensing and the construction of optical analogues in the laboratory.
