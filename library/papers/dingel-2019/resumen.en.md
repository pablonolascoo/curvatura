The authors present a photonic analogue of the relativistic aberration of light from special relativity, using an all-pass filter based on microring resonators, a basic building block of photonic integrated circuits. They study its phase response in detail and compare it with an earlier analogue inspired by the Thomas effect that used the same filter. The work strengthens the link between special relativity and photonic circuits and supports the idea of special relativity on a chip, with programmable circuits combining several analogues. It does not address propulsion but illustrates simulating relativistic effects in the laboratory.
