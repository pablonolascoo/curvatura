The article proposes an automatic method to detect and quantify exudates in fundus images associated with diabetic retinopathy. It separates exudates by thresholding, removes the optic disc with morphological operations and connected component analysis, and classifies images and severity with a fuzzy inference system. It reports sensitivity of 91.11 percent, specificity of 100 percent and accuracy of 93.84 percent. The goal is to reduce ophthalmologists' workload. It has no relation to physics or propulsion.
