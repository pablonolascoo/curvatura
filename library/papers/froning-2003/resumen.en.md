The author reviews his 1980 idea of a quantum interstellar ramjet, which would exploit the electromagnetic zero-point energy of the vacuum for relativistic flight to the stars. The paper briefly summarizes the proposal and updates it with later work by others on the nature of the quantum vacuum. It also discusses possibilities of interacting with the vacuum for propulsion and power and for reducing the vehicle's flight resistance. According to the sources, it is a conceptual review without numerical results, relevant to vacuum-energy-based propulsion.
