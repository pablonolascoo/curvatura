The paper reviews standard results on the initial value problem of general relativity in the presence of matter. It applies them to show, in a well-defined sense, that finite perturbations of the gravitational field travel no faster than light. It also concludes that it is impossible to construct a warp drive like Alcubierre's 1994 one without exotic matter. It thus gives a causality-based argument for the need for matter with exotic properties in such geometries.
