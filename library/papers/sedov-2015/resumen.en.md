The work studies the formation of small-amplitude, spatially localized oscillatory structures in an atomic Bose-Einstein condensate confined in two- and three-dimensional optical lattices. Using the k.p method, the authors show that the initial Gross-Pitaevskii equation can be mapped onto nonlinear Klein-Gordon equations. From this they predict the formation of breather-like oscillating localized states, which they call atomic oscillons. The associated topics are nonlinear hyperbolic metamaterials, which are sometimes used as spacetime analogues. Its link to propulsion is indirect.
