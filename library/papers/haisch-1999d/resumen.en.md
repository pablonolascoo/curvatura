This text, presented at a NASA workshop on breakthrough propulsion physics, summarises a propulsion idea based on the zero-point field. It recalls that Alcubierre's superluminal motion was judged unattainable by Pfenning and Ford. The hypothesis is that the inertia and gravitation of matter arise from electromagnetic interactions with that field. A new covariant derivation obtains the relativistic equation of motion from Maxwell's equations. According to the authors, this opens possibilities but rules out Bondi's negative inertial mass.
