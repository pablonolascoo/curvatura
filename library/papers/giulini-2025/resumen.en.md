These are notes and commentary, in German, on chapter III of Weyl's book on the relativity of space and time. They describe the historical path to special relativity, its geometric content, the group-theoretic treatment of Galilean spacetime and the use of four-dimensional tensor calculus. They include historical excursions and conceptual and mathematical deepenings that connect to current views. It is historical and foundational material, with no direct link to propulsion.
