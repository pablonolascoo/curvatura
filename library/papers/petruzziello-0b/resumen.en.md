The authors introduce a decoherence process caused by quantum gravity effects, assuming a foamy spacetime with a fluctuating minimal length that coincides on average with the Planck scale. With deformed commutation relations and a fluctuating deformation parameter, they derive a Lindblad master equation giving localization in energy space and decoherence times consistent with available evidence. The rate is minimal below the Planck scale and maximal in the mesoscopic regime. They propose tests with cavity optomechanics.
