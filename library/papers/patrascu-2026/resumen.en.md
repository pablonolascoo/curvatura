The paper takes a perspective in which quantum mechanics is discovered first and the causal structure of spacetime emerges from the correlation geometry of many-photon states. It uses the Uhlmann bundle to encode classical attributes of electromagnetic waves as quantum correlations, and introduces a correlation gauge that controls interference conditions. This allows superluminal group velocities as an emergent interference effect, distinct from the true front cone tied to microcausality. To alter the latter it proposes a correlation-dressed Maxwell theory in which, in a prepared sector, the local front velocity could exceed the vacuum one. It includes an experimental plan to test this.
