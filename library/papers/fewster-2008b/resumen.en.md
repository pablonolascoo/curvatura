These lecture notes introduce quantum field theory in curved spacetime, starting from the basics and reaching areas of current research. They cover globally hyperbolic spacetimes, canonical quantization, Euclidean Green functions, the Unruh effect and gravitational particle production. They also treat algebraic quantization, the Hadamard and microlocal spectrum conditions, and quantum energy inequalities. The latter bound how much negative energy quantum theory allows, which is central for assessing wormholes and warp drives.
