The work introduces a metric-free model of null dynamics in 3+1 dimensions, based on a single algebraic constraint that defines a null cone. Within it, it identifies a universal critical shear value marking the transition from divergence to refocusing of null trajectories, arising from the algebraic interaction between expansion and twist. It is invariant across three geometric embeddings, governs a bifurcation of the Jacobi equation and matches a phase transition in a PT-symmetric quantum analogue. It is a minimal analytic framework for pre-metric, optical and analogue settings.
