The work explores thermal effects on Casimir wormholes in higher-dimensional Einstein-Gauss-Bonnet gravity. It investigates how thermal fluctuations change the quantum vacuum energy density of the Casimir effect and what impact this has on the structure of traversable wormholes. It derives the shape function from the field equations with temperature-corrected Casimir energy and verifies the geometric conditions for formation, such as asymptotic flatness. Thermal corrections increase spatial curvature in the throat and influence traversability, energy conditions, stability and complexity.
