This is the introduction to a book on time travel. It presents its four parts: one on the different ways of travelling in time, one on some of the paradoxes, a third on the grandfather paradox and its proposed resolutions, and a last one on what the world would be like if impossibility theory were true. It also explains why the chapter declines to give an explicit definition of time travel. It is a philosophy text, only indirectly related to closed timelike curves.
