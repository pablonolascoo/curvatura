The article develops Real-Now-Front cosmology, a generative framework in which spacetime arises from aligning, slice by slice, a pre-geometric substrate of chronons, which are alignment degrees of freedom rather than quanta of time. Their coherent ordering induces an effective Lorentzian metric and causal structure, and the Lorentzian signature is selected by Cauchy stability. A temporal coherence principle produces kinematic cosmic expansion without vacuum energy and reproduces general relativity near equilibrium. An exclusivity principle generates non-singular cores that would be cold dark matter candidates. It is a theoretical proposal of emergent gravity.
