The Alcubierre warp drive is a controversial theory, and some arguments hold that it may not be physically possible. Building on earlier studies, this work suggests that extremely dense quark matter, with a low temperature limit reaching zero, could satisfy the requirements of the Alcubierre model when the color interaction potential energy is considered. The energy-momentum tensor and the velocity of the warp drive model supported by this quark matter are also described. It is an attempt to find a material source for the exotic energy the metric demands.
