The work compares two common ways of including doubly special relativity effects in static spherical black holes: modified dispersion relations on a fixed background and rainbow metrics. The authors show that, evaluated at the same operational energy scale, both give the same near-horizon rescaling of the Hawking temperature. They apply the result to the Amelino-Camelia and Magueijo-Smolin models and to a two-parameter family, and find that the correction depends on the chosen prescription. They also discuss implications for phenomenological bounds.
