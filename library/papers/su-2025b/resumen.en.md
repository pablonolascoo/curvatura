The authors propose a hexagonal-structure warp spacecraft with metamaterial plates of Alconi-CrAlconi alloy that would absorb Casimir energy with negative-matter properties. They start from the view that Alcubierre's theory has no physical impediment, and attribute the difficulty to the lack of energy sources. According to them, their earlier papers showed that Casimir energy behaves as exotic matter, with negative photons at 0.00206 K enclosed by a light sphere of positive photons in the outer shell. They claim that structures at the 0.1 nm scale could solve the problem. It is a speculative proposal.
