The article gives an overview of the Hypersphere World-Universe Model (WUM), which aims to unify and simplify existing cosmological models into a single picture of the origin, evolution, structure and fate of the universe. According to the author, it explains data such as the age of the world, critical density, the Hubble and gravitational parameters, cosmic background temperatures and macrostructure. It predicts masses of dark matter particles, photons and neutrinos, and proposes new interactions and a new fundamental parameter Q.
