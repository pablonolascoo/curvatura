This is a review of the efforts of the past two decades to understand how physics enforces the ordinary topology and causal structure we observe, from subnuclear to cosmological scales. It surveys the status of topological censorship and the topology of event horizons, chronology protection in classical and semiclassical gravity, and progress on quantum energy inequalities. It is relevant to whether nature forbids closed timelike curves and geometries such as wormholes or warp drives.
