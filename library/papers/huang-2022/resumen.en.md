The article argues that there is neither need nor reason to rely on general relativity for faster than light travel, because, in the author's view, the ban in special relativity is wrong. It criticizes the internal logic of general relativity and Einstein's field equation, which it considers derived by speculative reasoning. It claims that wormholes and warp drives require extreme spacetime bending and great negative energy that are not achievable. It proposes basic research, such as searching for faster than light particles with existing accelerators, and suggests negative energy is only an analytical tool.
