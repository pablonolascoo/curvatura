The chapter presents the design of an artificial intelligence-augmented developmental instruction simulation to improve contemplative practices in the face of complexity. It focuses on cognitive and moral reasoning to improve self-awareness. An AI tutoring aid accompanies participants during a simulated mission to Mars in an immersive 3D world, with tasks that develop these practices. Model-based reasoning tools with high visual, auditory and interactive fidelity are used. It is an educational technology work, unrelated to propulsion physics.
