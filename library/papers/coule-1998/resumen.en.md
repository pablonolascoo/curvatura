This short comment argues that Alcubierre's warp spacetime cannot be set up without first being able to distribute matter at tachyonic speed; put roughly, you need a warp drive to build one. It adds that over short distances, where the energy conditions may be violated, one could envision opening the light cones to raise the apparent speed of light. It is an early objection on grounds of causality and construction against the viability of the warp drive.
