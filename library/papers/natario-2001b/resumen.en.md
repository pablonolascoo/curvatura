The article challenges the common idea that Alcubierre's warp drive works by contracting space in front of the bubble and expanding it behind. The authors show that this contraction and expansion is only a marginal consequence of Alcubierre's choice and explicitly construct a similar spacetime in which neither contraction nor expansion occurs. Global and optical properties of warp-type spacetimes are also discussed. It is a classic reference on variants of the Alcubierre metric.
