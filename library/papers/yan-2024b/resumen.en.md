The work studies the transformation of Dirac equation Hamiltonians with quantum perturbation in two-dimensional black hole models. It derives the modified Rabi model Hamiltonians for a point black hole and a noncommutative black hole, and finds the velocity and acceleration perturbations of Dirac particles. From the Dirac equation solutions it obtains fluctuation equations for the charge and current densities in the two types of black hole. According to the results, the Hamiltonians contain correction terms with different powers of the creation and annihilation operators, and the accelerations have additional terms.
