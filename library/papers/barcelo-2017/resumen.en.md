The chapter considers a pre-emptive chronology protection mechanism that would destabilize superluminal warp drives through the back-reaction of quantum matter on the geometry. This would rule out even the conceptual possibility of building a time machine with them. It starts from the fact that general relativity admits solutions with faster-than-light motion and hence time travel. The result is examined in standard quantum field theory in curved spacetime and in a theory with Lorentz invariance breaking at high energies. It ends with lessons and future perspectives.
