The paper develops a Generalized Gauge Equation in a principal bundle with group GL(n,C) to unify the fundamental interactions geometrically, showing how electromagnetism can be mapped onto gravitational configurations through gauge transformations. It derives the relation between the Weyl and electromagnetic tensors with a coefficient 8 pi alpha fixed by variational analysis. It shows that two optical solitons transform into gravitational solitons through a rotational gauge transformation, corresponding to photon-graviton conversion in the weak field. These solitons would form localized curvature structures capable of superluminal propulsion, with derived effective velocities up to 3c.
