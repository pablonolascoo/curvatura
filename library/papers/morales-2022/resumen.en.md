The work starts from the fact that warp drives exist in general relativity, such as Alcubierre bubbles, with apparent faster-than-light travel. A result by Gao and Wald indicates this is unlikely if matter satisfies the null energy and null generic conditions, and Galloway generalized it to any gravity model unless there is a null line, an inextendible null geodesic with achronal image. That proof was never published, so the authors give an independent proof using the Raychaudhuri equation, avoiding several technical complications. They discuss its consequences, in particular for causality problems.
