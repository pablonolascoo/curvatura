The authors extend a spherical warp-type geometry by adding a non-trivial lapse function as an extra degree of freedom. This allows the system to hold an anisotropic fluid with heat flow, widening the kinds of matter considered for these bubbles. They solve the equations numerically and study how the energy conditions change once heat flux is included, for a generic observer. They then map the regions of the solution where those conditions are satisfied. The work matters for understanding what matter, and with which properties, a warp bubble would require.
