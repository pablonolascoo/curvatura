From an optical standpoint, the authors propose de Sitter space with a generalized Poincaré lens, based on the equivalence between a static curved spacetime and an isotropic optical medium. The familiar correspondence between de Sitter space and the Poincaré disk is a special case. They find that the generalized space has a topological cosmic string core at its center that deflects light in different ways, visible in the equivalent lens with a tunable horizon. They also introduce a one dimensional lens and, as an application, a multiple source illusion effect whose energy flow obeys the topological Poincaré-Hopf theorem.
