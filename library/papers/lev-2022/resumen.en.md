The paper presents a method based on the nonequilibrium statistical operator that accounts for the inhomogeneous particle distribution induced by gravitational interaction. With it, the thermodynamic relations of self-gravitating systems are obtained using classical physics only. According to the authors, the equations for the extremum of the partition function fully reproduce the equations of general relativity, so the classical statistical description and its thermodynamics lead to Einstein's gravitational equation. It is a theoretical approach to the thermodynamic origin of gravity.
