This book examines the faster-than-light motions that appear in special relativity. It shows that in all such cases subtle forces of nature prevent them from being used to send signals faster than light. It treats the topics both conceptually, with little or no mathematics, and quantitatively, relying on many illustrations. It is aimed at scientists familiar with the subject and at general readers. It is a useful reference on the limits of causality and superluminal signals, which frame any proposal for faster-than-light travel.
