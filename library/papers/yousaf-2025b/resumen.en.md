The work studies the effect of a component tied to a topological charge, a global monopole, on viable wormhole models in f(G) gravity, where G is the Gauss-Bonnet term. It considers a static spherically symmetric spacetime coupled to anisotropic matter and examines the structure of the wormholes and the matter that would support them with different shape functions, assessing their stability and energy conditions. It also computes an anisotropy parameter to see whether repulsive or attractive forces would arise. It concludes that the global monopole parameter strongly affects the energy requirements.
