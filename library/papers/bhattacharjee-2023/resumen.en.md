This preprint is a popular guide to rocket science, requiring no background, explaining rocket dynamics, propulsion, orbital mechanics and Kessler syndrome with pictures and historical facts. It argues that rockets are not the future for reaching the stars and moves on to electrohydrodynamic propulsion and antigravity, covering various engines and experiments. Topics include warping space and keeping a constant 1g gravity. The antigravity claims are speculative, and the sources give no quantitative evidence.
