The article describes explanatory and illustrative visualization methods for communicating special and general relativity, their geometric structure and the related cosmology and astrophysics, aimed at a general audience. It discusses strategies based on physics and mathematics education and which methods work for each medium, such as magazines, television, talks or interactive museum exhibits. The main approach is egocentric: the recipient takes part in a visually enriched thought experiment, sometimes combined with outside views. It adds technical novelties such as GPU relativistic rendering, 4D ray tracing and interactive gravitational deflection, and checks usefulness with feedback from recipients and collaborators.
