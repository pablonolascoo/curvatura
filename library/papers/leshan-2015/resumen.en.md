The author proposes a gravitational propulsion concept, called "Hole Levitation", that would use artificial gravity generated by vacuum holes. It resembles a gravitational slingshot but without needing planets or complicated manoeuvres. The artificial gravity source accelerates the vehicle in one direction and the surrounding medium in the opposite one, so it is not a reactionless drive: momentum is taken from nearby stars and planets and is conserved overall. The generator could also damp inertial forces. It is a conceptual proposal with no experimental data.
