This short review starts from the so-called Havana syndrome, which occurred in Cuba around 2016 and 2017, when American diplomats fell ill without a clear cause. It mentions that agencies only say the source is not natural and a microwave beam weapon is suspected. The author offers a different perspective on directed energy weapons, which are not new and which national laboratories and universities have long been developing. It is a text on military technology, unrelated to spacetime physics.
