The work seeks electrically charged traversable wormholes in linear teleparallel gravity coupled to Euler-Heisenberg nonlinear electrodynamics, to reduce the need for exotic matter. Using a static spherically symmetric spacetime with constant redshift, it obtains an analytic solution in which torsion, charge and nonlinear electromagnetic corrections all contribute to the structure. It also studies light deflection and echo time. Its interest is exploring modified theories that improve the physical viability of such wormholes.
