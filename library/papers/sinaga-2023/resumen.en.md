This is a theoretical study in one space plus one time dimension reviewing essential concepts of quantum mechanics and theoretical physics. It examines the Dirac equation for spin-1/2 particles, the time-dependent Schrödinger equation with flat conformal metric and its link to Dirac. It also explores the Alcubierre metric, particle modelling in a harmonic potential and the Gödel solution as an example of spacetime peculiarities. The authors state there are no experiments or practical applications, only theoretical analysis.
