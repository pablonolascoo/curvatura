The work presents a model of fermion condensation in a flat FRW background. It shows that condensation can occur through the BCS mechanism due to a four-fermion interaction that appears naturally when fermions are included in gravity. The authors argue that this process could be the basis of a non-perturbative resolution of the cosmological constant problem. To connect with observations, they show that WMAP3 cosmic microwave background data can be fitted by a model with zero effective cosmological constant if the universe is riddled with voids of order 100 Mpc, modelled with LTB metrics.
