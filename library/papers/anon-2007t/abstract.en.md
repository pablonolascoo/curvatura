This paper studies the two-dimensional Brans-Dicke cosmological model with exotic matter and dark energy interaction, and obtains some new cosmological solutions that can be used to explain the accelerated expansion of the universe; in addition, the cosmological solutions in the two-dimensional Brans-Dicke string gravity model are discussed, and it is found that the string gas has the peculiar property of negative energy density, a result that can lead to inflation in the early universe.
