The work explains why purely scalar theories of gravitation predict a PPN parameter equal to zero and hence only half the observed light deflection: in a real scalar wave equation the propagation speed cannot depend on the background field. It proposes a vacuum as a complex superfluid with a relativistic logarithmic nonlinearity, analyzed with the Madelung transformation, which adds a density-dependent sound speed and a macroscopic flow. Using the acoustic metric formalism it computes the PPN parameter and ties it to the variation of that speed.
