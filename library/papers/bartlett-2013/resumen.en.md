This is an opinion essay arguing that Einstein, rather than Englert and Higgs, deserves the 2013 Nobel Prize in Physics for the theory of how particles acquire mass. The author accepts the work on the Higgs field but holds that CERN has only confirmed the particle's existence, not its role in mass. He draws on an article of his own, submitted to a journal, inspired by Vilenkin's ideas on eternal inflation and quantum creation of the universe from the quantum vacuum. It is a speculative text and the sources show no calculations or verifiable data of its own.
