The study couples a hyperbolic potential and the conformal Liouville potential to the phonon field in two-dimensional gravity, within an acoustic black hole model. It calculates the horizon coordinate, Hawking temperature, phonon mass and fluid velocity, and how they change with the field coefficient and the central charge. With the hyperbolic potential, the phonon resembles the Higgs particle: a horizon of about 10 µm, a Hawking temperature of 1 nK and a fluid speed of 1 mm/s. The Liouville model gives similar orders of magnitude.
