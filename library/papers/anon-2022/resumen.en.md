The paper considers the possibility of producing on a body a repulsive force, opposite to gravitational attraction, inside a nonuniform gravitational field. Unlike the reaction force of a rocket, it would not require ejecting mass or shedding momentum. The necessary condition is that the gravitational field be inhomogeneous. The author calls it a "quasi antigravitational" force and stresses that it would matter for moving through empty space. The available sources do not detail the specific model or give numbers.
