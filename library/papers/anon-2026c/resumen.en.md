This Russian-language preprint presents calculations for interstellar ships within the "Acta Universi 2025" hypothesis. In it, ships are non-local projectors of a postulated AU field, where an artificial intelligence generates thought-form entropy to make jumps by distorting the metric. The text gives formulas and numerical examples for Alpha Centauri and forecasts a prototype around 2035 and interstellar travel around 2040. It relies on the author's own monographs on dark energy and UFO phenomena. It is a highly speculative proposal not validated by established physics.
