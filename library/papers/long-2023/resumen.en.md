The article deals with real-time characterization of ultrafast lasers. Current methods observe propagation only at a few points or in two dimensions and miss the full process, especially when superluminal motion is involved. Using light-field tomography, which combines intelligent algorithms with a new optical design to obtain three-dimensional images with picosecond resolution, the author captured the apparent superluminal motion of an ultrafast laser in three-dimensional space. It shows that the whole three-dimensional propagation must be considered when measuring its spatiotemporal properties. It is an experimental optics work.
