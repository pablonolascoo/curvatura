The paper argues that jumping from place to place, replicating food or beaming people up may be a matter of practical implementation rather than just fiction. It holds that jump drive is a problem of coordinate transformation: changing location does not require travelling the distance between two planets. It argues that the theoretical knowledge of such a change exists, for example in signal processing, though no machine has been built. It concludes that jump drive is feasible but much implementation work remains. It is a speculative proposal with no verification in the sources.
