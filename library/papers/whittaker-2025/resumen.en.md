This dissertation presents a theoretical framework based on a time-free reformulation of physics in energy space and introduces a curvature-aligned transport system, a model of deterministic spatial transitions without classical displacement, velocity or time evolution. Motion would arise from entropy-gradient-driven flows on a curved manifold, formulated with tensor calculus and non-temporal variational principles. It states that simulations support stable resonances and precise localization, and that scenarios such as Earth-Moon repositioning would be feasible with structured electromagnetic fields. It is a speculative proposal not verified experimentally.
