This chapter applies stochastic gravity to the backreaction problem in cosmology when the gravitational field couples to a quantum conformal matter field. Using the CTP functional formalism, the authors derive the Einstein-Langevin equations for metric fluctuations on a weakly perturbed, spatially flat Friedmann-Lemaitre-Robertson-Walker universe, and obtain the probability of particle creation. They also derive the renormalized expectation value of the quantum stress-energy tensor and the corresponding semiclassical Einstein equation. It provides tools for treating quantum energy in gravity.
