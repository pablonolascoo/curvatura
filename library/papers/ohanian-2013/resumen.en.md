A textbook chapter on black holes and gravitational collapse. It starts from the dimensionless quantity GM/rc², a measure of the strength of the gravitational field, small in the Solar System and of order one near an extremely compact mass. It describes how at r equal to 3GM/c² light can orbit in a circle in the Schwarzschild field and how at 2GM/c² nothing escapes, not even a spacecraft with powerful engines. It is general relativity background for understanding horizons and strong fields.
