The paper gives the first description of dynamical analogue spacetimes up to arbitrary order of nonlinear perturbation about stationary solutions of hydrodynamic systems. In analogue gravity, fluid fluctuations appear as a massless scalar field on a curved spacetime. The authors find a high-frequency response with known properties of perturbed black holes and a low-frequency regime with novel features. The framework applies to nonlinear waves in compressible fluids as analogues of phenomena on black hole spacetimes.
