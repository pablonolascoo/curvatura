The authors study the acoustic geometry of axially symmetric accretion in vertical hydrostatic equilibrium around a Kerr black hole, and how it depends on the background and on two expressions for the flow geometry. They first find the sonic points and the stationary shock between them. They then perturb the flow to obtain the metric elements of the acoustic spacetime. They compute the acoustic surface gravity as a function of the black hole's spin for several flow thicknesses. It is an analogue-gravity study of acoustic black holes.
