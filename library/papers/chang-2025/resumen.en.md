The work analyses the geometry of the static black bottle spacetime by studying the geodesic motion of photons. It derives the geodesic equations with the Hamilton-Jacobi formalism and classifies the orbits by their conserved quantities, using effective potentials to visualize the allowed trajectories. The classification changes with the acceleration parameter of the spacetime. It finds analytical solutions in terms of Jacobi elliptic functions and draws the geodesics through isometric embedding alongside the horizons. It is a general relativity study of null geodesics, a useful tool for analysing exotic metrics.
