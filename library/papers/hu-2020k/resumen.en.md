The chapter studies the stress-energy bitensor and its symmetrized product with two goals: presenting the point-separation regularization method and using it to compute the noise kernel, the correlation function of that bitensor. It introduces the geometric tools for bitensors, regularizes the ultraviolet divergences of the quantum stress-energy tensor in curved spacetimes and derives the noise kernel using Green functions. It shows its trace vanishes for a massless conformal field and computes it in de Sitter space, useful for back-reaction and fluctuations of quantum fields.
