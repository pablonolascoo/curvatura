The article discusses the hints that continuum space and time disappear at microscopic scales, whether through a discrete nature or a fundamental nonlocality in a quantum theory of gravity, and how these ideas are realized in specific approaches. It then turns the problem around: continuum spacetime would emerge from the collective behaviour of discrete pre-geometric atoms of quantum space, as a kind of condensate, through a phase transition called geometrogenesis. As a concrete example it outlines the group field theory framework and a tentative procedure for emergence, and re-examines the conceptual issues it raises.
