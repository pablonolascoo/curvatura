Starting from Ford and Pfenning's uncertainty-type argument that a regular 100 m warp bubble is unrealistic, and Van Den Broeck's improvement with a bubble of small surface area and large volume, the authors modify that idea. They find new lower bounds on warp drive energy by varying parameters not explored before. They also consider micro warp bubbles as physical entities in their own right, which could transmit information faster than light and might have formed spontaneously after the Big Bang.
