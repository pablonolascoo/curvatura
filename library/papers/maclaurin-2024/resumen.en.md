The article investigates timelike and null geodesics in the rotating time machine spacetime proposed by Ralph and collaborators in 2020, a rotating analogue of the Alcubierre warp drive. The authors obtain geodesics that begin and end in the surrounding flat region and yet achieve time travel relative to static observers there. This is a global property, since the geodesics remain locally future-pointing and timelike or null. The result connects warp-type geometries with closed timelike curves and causality.
