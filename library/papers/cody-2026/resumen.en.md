The article proposes to interpret spacetime as a substrate of finite strength that supports metric relations up to a critical stress threshold. The Kretschmann curvature invariant is reinterpreted as substrate stress, and once the threshold is reached the continuum fails and the medium moves to a non-metric phase instead of infinite curvature. Event horizons would be mechanical failure boundaries where the geometric description ends. The external predictions of general relativity are unchanged. The framework brings together geometric, thermodynamic and mechanical views as a way to avoid singularities.
