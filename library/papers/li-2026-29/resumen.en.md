The authors construct new black holes through curvature-induced scalarization in Einstein gravity coupled to a massless phantom scalar field. They show that general wormhole solutions with independent scalar charge can turn into a black hole with secondary scalar hair depending only on the mass. The long-range force between two identical black holes can be attractive, zero or repulsive depending on the relation between mass and scalar charge, and the mass can be negative. They suggest the exotic matter behind wormholes can also produce exotic black holes.
