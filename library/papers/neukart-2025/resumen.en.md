This article analyzes interstellar travel in its technological, ethical and sociopolitical dimensions. It reviews advances in propulsion systems, habitat construction and life support alongside the ethical, political and philosophical questions raised by human presence on other worlds. It argues for an integrative framework combining scientific progress with an ethical commitment to responsible exploration, environmental care and respect for possible extraterrestrial ecosystems. It presents interstellar exploration as both a technical challenge and a philosophical journey, guided by caution and humility. It is a synthesis and offers no calculations.
