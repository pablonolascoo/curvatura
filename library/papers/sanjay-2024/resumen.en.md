The article studies traversable wormhole models in Finsler geometry, with a shape function and a suitable choice of redshift function. The solutions for the shape function meet the metric criteria demanded in other works. With an anisotropic energy-momentum tensor, the validity of the solution is checked against all the energy conditions. The authors also claim to obtain a solution without exotic matter thanks to the redshift function, and present graphical results and the anisotropy factor.
