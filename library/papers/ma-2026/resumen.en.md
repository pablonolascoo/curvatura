This review explains how vortices and solitons form and are controlled in exciton-polariton superfluids and condensates, hybrid light-matter quasiparticles in semiconductor microstructures. An optical beam can drive polaritons into nonlinear states with quantised phase jumps and stationary shapes. It focuses on spinor polaritons, where vortices with half-integer topological charge can arise. Applications mentioned include information processing, all-optical circuits and quantum computing. It is nonlinear optics and condensed matter, unrelated to propulsion.
