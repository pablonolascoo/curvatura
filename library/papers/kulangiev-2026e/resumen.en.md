The work explains why purely scalar theories of gravity predict only half the observed light deflection, and studies whether a vacuum modelled as a superfluid with logarithmic nonlinearity can fix this. Using the Madelung transformation it obtains a sound speed that depends on local density and computes the PPN parameter gamma. It concludes that no static barotropic fluid gives gamma equal to 1 with nonzero deflection, but that an acoustic metric with macroscopic flow, the Painleve-Gullstrand profile, does. An accretion calculation does not generate that flow, and deriving it from microscopic dynamics remains open.
