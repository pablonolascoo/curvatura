The paper starts from the view that the metric tied to gravitation is only somewhat distorted by enormous electromagnetic fields, and that there is no strong coupling between gravity and ordinary electromagnetism because they differ in nature. It suggests that if ordinary electromagnetic fields could be given an essence and form similar to the gravitational field, the coupling would be much stronger and useful for propulsion. It describes several ways to create specially conditioned electromagnetic radiation and how its fields might interact with those of gravity. It reviews experiments already performed and more complete tests planned.
