Quantum energy inequalities limit how negative the renormalized energy density of a quantum field can be when averaged along a timelike curve. In globally hyperbolic spacetimes the massive Klein-Gordon field obeys a difference inequality that depends on a chosen Hadamard reference state and often cannot be evaluated. The authors obtain the first absolute inequality in four dimensions, whose bound depends only on local geometry, by adapting the earlier proof and using the Sobolev wave-front set. The bound is explicit, can be formulated covariantly under extra conditions, and extends to adiabatic states.
