The text recalls that solutions of Einstein's equations with negative energy densities, that is violating the weak energy condition, have been obtained: traversable wormholes, the Alcubierre warp drive and the Krasnikov tube. All relate to superluminal travel, although locally the speed of light is not exceeded. Defining faster than light travel in generic spacetimes is hard, and one can build metrics that seem to allow it but are in fact flat Minkowski space. Hence it is important to give an appropriate definition of superluminal travel.
