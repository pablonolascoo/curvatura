The paper builds a cyclically symmetric, non-vacuum spacetime that admits closed timelike curves appearing after a certain moment, in other words a time machine. It is a four-dimensional extension of Misner space in curved spacetime. The author states that the solution is asymptotically flat, free of curvature singularities and of Petrov type II. The matter content is pure radiation, which satisfies the energy condition. It is relevant to the study of causality and of geometries that allow time travel.
