Starting from the idea of analogue spacetimes, the authors develop an "analogue transformation acoustics" formalism that allows transformations mixing space and time or involving moving fluids, which was impossible with the standard approach. They illustrate it by designing acoustic versions of a dynamic compressor and a spacetime cloak. They state that the method can be generalized to other physical fields whose equations are not form invariant, with possible applications in quantum fluids and graphene electronics.
