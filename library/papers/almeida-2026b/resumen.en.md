The paper studies the Dirac Hamiltonian when the mass varies in space and asks which operator ordering is consistent. Unlike the non-relativistic case, only one Hermitian ordering preserves probability-current conservation. This requirement adds a term proportional to the logarithmic gradient of the mass, which modifies the effective kinetic operator and deforms the spectral quantization condition. In a compact geometry, an analytic calculation gives a second-order, mode-dependent spectral shift that grows strongly near the mass-inversion threshold. It is background theoretical physics with no direct link to propulsion.
