The article proposes extracting thrust from the excited quantum vacuum using squeezed light, as a basis for field propulsion. It explains that by manipulating the field one can lower the local energy density below its vacuum-state value, and that to reach a steady state the vacuum structure ceases to be homogeneous. Squeezed light generates squeezed vacuum states and a coordination geometry of the energy density, helped by advances in nonlinear optics. The author obtains the theoretical equation of the force associated with the vacuum perturbation and identifies several supporting experimental concepts. It is a theoretical proposal without experimental demonstration.
