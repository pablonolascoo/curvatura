The study has two parts aimed at making nuclear pulse propulsion realistic. The first describes a viable way of inertial confinement fusion, of magneto-inertial type, in which heavy nuclei accelerated by Lorentz forces achieve the confinement and a special geometry gives the reaction spherical symmetry. The second explains how to direct the nuclear explosion and turn it into thrust using magnetic forces, with the strong field obtained from the energy of the explosion itself through an advanced explosively compressed magnetic flux generator.
