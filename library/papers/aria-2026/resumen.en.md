The work proposes a theoretical framework for photonic quantum systems in curved time, where static Gaussian acceleration fields make the clock rate depend on position and produce discrete temporal states. It solves a curvature-modified Schrödinger equation for surface acoustic wave and quartz resonators, obtaining temporal qubits and logic operations without spatial wiring. Simulations indicate that graphene keeps states delocalised, while carbon nanotubes and MoS2 confine them. It is a quantum computing proposal with little direct connection to propulsion.
