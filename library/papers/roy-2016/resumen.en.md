The article offers a mathematical visualisation of the relativity among space, time and height, following Einstein's special relativity. It focuses on how time varies as space and height increase or decrease. It treats space, time and height as three independent dimensions, derives several expressions and plots them in three-dimensional graphs. The author concludes that the relativity of time also exists in combination with both space and height at once. It is a brief, non-standard commentary with no comparison to established theory.
