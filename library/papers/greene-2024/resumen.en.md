The work presents a theoretical warp bubble model that allows faster-than-light travel with positive energy densities, instead of the exotic negative energy of traditional models. It builds on Eric Lentz's framework and introduces a non-uniform energy distribution for better control of the field. Using numerical simulations it argues that the bubble is stable and feasible. A key finding is anisotropic behaviour that would allow the direction of the field to be tuned and the energy demand to be reduced.
