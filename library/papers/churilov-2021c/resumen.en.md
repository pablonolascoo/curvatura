The authors study the amplification of shallow-water waves traveling along a duct of variable cross section with a nonuniform flow. They derive the basic equations and analyze the solutions asymptotically near critical points, where wave speed equals current speed, a kinematic analogue of black and white hole horizons. With two horizons, the region between them mimics a wormhole. Numerical calculations show that amplification occurs only in the black hole followed by white hole arrangement, with a factor depending on frequency and on the velocity ratio. It is a hydrodynamic analogue of wormholes.
