The text argues that Einstein's understanding of gravity misled modern physics, while his contemporaries had the correct one. It reviews the concept of gravity in light of Vedic nuclear physics and the proposals of H. A. Wilson, Puthoff, Alcubierre, Davis, Dicke and the Hoyle-Narlikar theory. Finally it explains an alleged substratum tunnelling effect, or wormhole, that would make interstellar and intergalactic travel possible. The author claims it provides the concepts needed for interstellar travel in the near future. It is a speculative proposal outside established physics.
