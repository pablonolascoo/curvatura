This joint editorial voices concern about scientism, the excessive belief in the power of scientific knowledge, which leads to labelling some topics as acceptable and others as heretical. The authors point out that orthodoxy is only the majority view of present-day experts and is not guaranteed to reflect reality. They criticise the denigration of everything unconventional as if the dominant science were always reliable. They argue that fringe science is a tautology rather than a pariah. It is an opinion piece on the philosophy and sociology of science, with no physical data.
