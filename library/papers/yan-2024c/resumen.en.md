The Hamiltonian mapping of the Dirac equation with a quantum perturbation is investigated in two-dimensional black hole models: the point-matter and the noncommutative ones. The author obtains the Hamiltonians of a modified Rabi model and perturbed expressions for the velocity and acceleration of the simulated Dirac particles, plus fluctuation equations for charge and current density. The Hamiltonians contain correction terms with different powers of creation and annihilation operators, features that could distinguish types of black holes in analogue gravity models.
