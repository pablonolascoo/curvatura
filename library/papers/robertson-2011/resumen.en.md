The article argues that rocket science lacks a space propulsion science discipline, one that develops new physical theories and models, and that this lack holds back ideas toward new frontiers and implies stagnation. According to the authors, conventional disciplines ignore the physics of acceleration, including the nature of gravity and inertia, needed for progress in spaceflight. It discusses various topics to argue that space propulsion science must become a major player if future space exploration is to reach new frontiers. It is an opinion and forward-looking text.
