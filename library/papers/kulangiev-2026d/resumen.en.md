The article starts from the fact that purely scalar gravitational theories give the PPN parameter gamma equal to zero and therefore only half of the observed light deflection. The authors trace the failure to the fact that the propagation speed of a real scalar wave equation cannot depend on the background field. They model the vacuum as a complex superfluid with relativistic logarithmic nonlinearity, using the Madelung transformation and the acoustic metric of Barceló, Liberati and Visser, which introduces a density-dependent sound speed and a macroscopic flow. They conclude that the static acoustic metric is ill-defined.
