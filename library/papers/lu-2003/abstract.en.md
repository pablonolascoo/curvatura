Recent many physicists suggest that the dark energy in the universe might result from the Born-Infeld(B-I) type scalar field of string theory. The universe of B-I type scalar field with potential can undergo a phase of accelerating expansion. The corresponding equation of state parameter lies in the range of $\displaystyle -1<ω<-{1/3}$. The equation of state parameter of B-I type scalar field without potential lies in the range of $0\leqω\leq1$. We find that weak energy condition and strong energy condition are violated for phantom B-I type scalar field. The equation of state parameter lies in the range of $ω<-1$.
