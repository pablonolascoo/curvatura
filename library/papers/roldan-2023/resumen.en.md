The article proposes a transformation factor for superluminal motion that symmetrically preserves spacetime intervals. It argues that if v exceeded c, the Lorentz transformations would need rewriting with the absolute value of x' expressed as γ(vt minus x), and that under the postulates of relativity the subluminal case gives the usual Lorentz factor. The superluminal factor is also invariant in Minkowski space. The authors stress it is not evidence that superluminal phenomena exist, but a new speculative argument.
