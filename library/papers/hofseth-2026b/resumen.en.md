This report proposes a theoretical synthesis between Eric Weinstein's Geometric Unity, which operates at the Planck scale, and the Refractive Vacuum Gravity (RVG) framework, oriented to metric engineering at much lower energy scales. It argues that RVG is not a competitor to Geometric Unity but would be its low-energy effective field theory. It is a conceptual proposal with no experimental results in the sources, linked to the idea of manipulating the spacetime metric.
