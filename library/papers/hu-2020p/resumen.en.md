The chapter presents the foundations of stochastic gravity, based on the Einstein-Langevin equation, which extends semiclassical gravity by adding sources due to the noise kernel, a bitensor describing fluctuations of the quantum stress-energy tensor. The equation is introduced through an axiomatic approach, as a self-consistent extension of semiclassical gravity, and through a functional approach based on the Feynman-Vernon influence functional and the open quantum system paradigm. It discusses the equivalence with quantum correlation functions in the 1/N expansion and proposes a criterion for the validity of semiclassical gravity.
