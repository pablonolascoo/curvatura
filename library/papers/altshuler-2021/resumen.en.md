This article reviews the work of Andrei Sakharov, from his engineering contributions to the first Soviet thermonuclear charges to fundamental physics. It describes the present state of fields that grew from his ideas: controlled fusion, magnetic cumulation and explosive generators, induced gravity, baryon acoustic oscillations and the baryon asymmetry of the universe. It also discusses the pulsating universe model and other topics he cared about, such as quantum cosmology and the anthropic principle, which are again at the forefront. It is mainly of historical and contextual value, with little direct link to propulsion.
