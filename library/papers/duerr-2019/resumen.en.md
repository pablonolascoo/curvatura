This philosophical paper revisits the debate on whether gravitational energy exists in any meaningful way in general relativity. It re-examines the arguments of Hoefer's eliminativism and the realists' replies, and points out where the real challenges for realism lie. It then critically analyzes recent proposals by Lam and Read, especially Read's use of functionalism borrowed from the philosophy of mind. It argues against the idea of a "functional gravitational energy". It matters because how energy is defined in gravity shapes debates about negative energy.
