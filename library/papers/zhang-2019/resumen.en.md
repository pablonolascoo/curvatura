The authors develop a metric-torsion theory for chiral structures using a generalized transformation optics framework. They show that chirality is uniquely determined by a metric with a local rotational degree of freedom. By analogy with the dislocation continuum, chirality can be seen as the torsion tensor of a Riemann-Cartan space, mimicked by the anholonomy of the orthonormal basis. As a demonstration they prove the equivalence of typical three-dimensional chiral metamaterials in the continuum limit, and give an analytical recipe for designing optical chirality.
