The article examines the feasibility of warp drives and their theoretical basis. A literature search finds three basic schemes for traveling faster than light, all of which violate parts of special relativity and require a negative stress-energy tensor, suggesting exotic matter in unrealistic amounts for exploring interstellar space. The author calls for new formulations and experiments to check whether these limitations are real. As a possible remedy, he suggests electromagnetic fields could produce an effect comparable to exotic matter, and that a Yilmaz-like approach could help with shortcomings of Einstein's gravity. The goal is to put these ideas on a firmer scientific footing.
