The article seeks a theory of gravity with both sides of the Einstein equation geometric, for which it proposes a coordinate-independent measure of time: the upper limit of measurable time from an event back to the big bang singularity, which builds a scalar field of time. Since time is measured by material clocks, it asks what maximal time a microscopic clock can measure between the big bang and an event inside an atomic nucleus, and whether it follows a geodesic or not inside matter. It is theoretical and conceptual work on maximum proper time, with no numerical results in the sources.
