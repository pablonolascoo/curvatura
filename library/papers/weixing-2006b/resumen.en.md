The paper obtains a lower bound on the spacetime-weighted average of the energy density of a scalar field in four-dimensional flat space. The bound takes the form of a quantum inequality, does not depend on the quantum state and relates only to the sampling functions, assumed smooth, positive and compactly supported. It turns out to equal the temporal quantum energy inequality. When the characteristic length of the temporal sampling tends to zero, the bound diverges, consistent with there being no spatial restriction on negative energy density. It is a key result for limiting negative energy.
