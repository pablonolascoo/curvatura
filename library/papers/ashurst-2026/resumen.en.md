The work proposes a subluminal constant-velocity warp shell of the type published by Fuchs and collaborators, with the same warp shift and passenger room but only 21% of the published mass: about 0.49 Jupiter masses for a 20 m outer radius, against 2.365. It proves that the null, weak, strong and dominant energy conditions hold for every observer at every point, using S-lemma matrix inequalities checked with interval arithmetic, and exact algebra in the flat interior and the Schwarzschild exterior. Two independent implementations corroborate it. It is a machine-verified result, not peer reviewed, within classical general relativity.
