The work builds on an earlier paper in which the Painlevé-Gullstrand flow regularizes the static metric pathology of a logarithmic superfluid vacuum. Here that flow is analyzed as a collective effect of N microscopic vortex-Gausson solitons and self consistency conditions are required, which the PG flow meets and the static solution does not. Assuming each soliton sources the acoustic metric through a localized perturbation, an emergent gravitational constant is derived that is independent of mass and distance. Matching it to Newton's constant gives a healing length of the order of the Planck length and a density of the order of the Planck density. The continuity constraint is left open.
