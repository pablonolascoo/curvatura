Using a simple conceptual analysis and a few numerical examples, the authors argue that traveling faster than light, or transmitting information instantly, produces temporal paradoxes and possible causality violations. Since this would imply that superluminal travel is impossible, they raise as an alternative a privileged reference frame, in the line of Lorentz and Poincaré, with an absolute metric for time and space. In that setting length contraction and time dilation would be real and the constancy of the speed of light only apparent. This would require a fundamental framework through which waves and particles propagate.
