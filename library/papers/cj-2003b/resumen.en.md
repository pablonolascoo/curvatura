The authors formulate a quantum weak energy inequality for general dynamical systems on static spacetimes, as a stability condition on states. They show that if a class of states satisfying it exists, then passive states exist, such as mixtures of ground and thermal states, and so the second law of thermodynamics holds. As a model they use the free scalar field on a static spacetime, with an algebra related to the Weyl algebra and Hadamard states. It is relevant to the limits on negative energy allowed by quantum theory.
