The paper analyzes traversable wormhole geometries in f(Q,T) gravity, where Q is the non-metricity scalar and T the trace of the energy-momentum tensor. It studies four functional forms (linear, quadratic, product and exponential) and introduces two new shape functions, an exponential relaxation one and one with an oscillatory correction. For specific negative couplings, the null energy condition holds at the throat, so exotic matter would not be needed. The volume integral quantifier is minimal in the quadratic model (0.08), versus 1.45 for positive coupling. Stability with the modified TOV equation is consistent, and gravitational lensing signatures are also studied.
