The work re-examines the dismissal of inertial propulsion as a violation of Newton's third law. It argues the paradox stems from an incomplete application of momentum conservation, which assumes rigid bodies and overlooks the geometry of internal forces. By analyzing systems with cyclic, non-collinear internal components, it shows the center of mass can translate without external interaction through internal momentum exchange. From there it outlines a range of architectures, from mechanical to electromagnetic analogues and finally inertial warp drives. It is a theoretical proposal.
