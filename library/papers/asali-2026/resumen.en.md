The authors introduce perfect fluid spacetimes, solitons and concircular vector fields on a statistical manifold. They study geometric and physical properties of these spacetimes within the framework of almost statistical solitons. They prove results on the divergence of the soliton vector fields and give explicit relations between differentiable functions and conditions for concircular fields. They also analyze how these solitons interact with the statistical Ricci curvature, obtaining constraints on scalar fields and curvature tensors. It is differential geometry mathematics, with only an indirect link to relativity.
