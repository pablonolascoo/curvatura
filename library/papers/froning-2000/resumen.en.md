This work suggests that ordinary electromagnetic fields barely couple to the fields underlying gravity and inertia because they are of a different nature. It proposes that a much stronger electro-gravitic coupling, useful for propulsion, might be achieved by conditioning electromagnetic fields into configurations resembling those of gravity and inertia. The paper summarizes the theoretical and experimental work done on this field conditioning. It is a propulsion proposal based on modifying gravity and inertia, and the sources give no numerical results.
