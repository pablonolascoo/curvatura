The authors study spin-1/2 fields in the (2+1)-dimensional draining bathtub spacetime, an analogue model of rotating black holes in hydrodynamic systems, with a draining parameter fixing the acoustic horizon and a circulation parameter giving frame dragging. They first analyse null and timelike geodesics to find the causal structure, critical orbits and capture regions. They then solve the radial Dirac equation with the triad formalism, for massless and massive fermions. They find spin-dependent modifications of angular momentum and distinct superradiant scattering regimes, and compute Dirac currents and the absorption length.
