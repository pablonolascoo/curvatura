The article proposes removing the need for exotic matter in the Alcubierre warp drive through generalized gauge transformations that directly relate the electromagnetic tensor to the Weyl tensor. By manipulating electromagnetic fields, curvature would be controlled, compressing space ahead of the ship and expanding it behind. For a 20-meter-diameter bubble it estimates an energy of about 4.9 times 10 to the 6 joules, against about 10 to the 62 joules for traditional models. It also discusses causality and time travel. These are theoretical claims without experimental verification.
