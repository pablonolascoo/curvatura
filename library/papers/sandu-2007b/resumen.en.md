This is the first of three papers presenting the elementary theory and design of a new type of spacecraft, the sonic electromagnetic gravitational spacecraft, and it sets out its principles. The authors claim it does not rely on momentum conservation for propulsion. It rests on four concepts: a theory of sonics on manipulating energy in wave form, the radiant hull integrated into the propulsion, and the use of high-intensity artificial gravitation, supported by the equivalence principle between inertia and a gravitational field. It is a conceptual proposal, and the sources show no experimental evidence.
