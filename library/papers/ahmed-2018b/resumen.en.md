A family of exact type N solutions of Einstein's equations with a negative cosmological constant is presented. The solutions contain closed timelike curves that appear after a certain moment in time. The spacetime has no curvature divergences and admits a null geodesic congruence without expansion, twist or shear, within the class of Kundt metrics. Its properties differ from those of the known plane-fronted gravitational waves. The family generalizes the two-dimensional Misner space to four dimensions. It serves as an example of causality violation, a theme linked to superluminal travel and wormholes.
