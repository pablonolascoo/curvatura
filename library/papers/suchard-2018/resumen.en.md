The paper proposes describing a universal scalar field of time, though not a universal time coordinate, and attributing its non-geodesic alignment to electromagnetic phenomena. According to the author, not only mass but also electric charge generates gravity, with charge coupled to a non-geodesic vector field. The non-geodesic acceleration is described with a generalisation of the Reeb vector. The text also discusses particle mass ratios and the fine-structure constant. It is a speculative theoretical proposal; the sources show no test against data.
