Travelling to neighboring stars as practically as in science fiction requires scientific breakthroughs, one of them a self contained propulsion that uses no propellant. To chart a path, the paper presents seven hypothetical space drives that illustrate the unsolved challenges and associated research objectives. These include finding a way to interact asymmetrically with the electromagnetic fluctuations of the vacuum, developing a physics describing inertia, gravity or spacetime as a function of electromagnetics, and determining whether negative mass exists. As an alternative, formalizing Mach's principle or reformulating ether concepts.
