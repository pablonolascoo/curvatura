The author proposes a phenomenological framework to explain the mismatch between galactic rotation curves and visible mass without dark matter. The idea is that the vacuum behaves as a compliant medium falling radially, which makes radial coordinates appear compressed to distant observers (the Mezzi effect). Using Newtonian dynamics in a true frame and a single compliance parameter, he reconstructs galaxies shell by shell and tests it on 175 late-type SPARC galaxies. It reproduces rotation curves with a root-mean-square residual of about 34 km/s and recovers the radial acceleration relation. It has little direct bearing on warp drives.
