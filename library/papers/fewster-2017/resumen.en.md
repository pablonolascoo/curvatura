This chapter reviews quantum energy inequalities, lower bounds on local averages of energy density in quantum field theory that replace the classical energy conditions of general relativity. It shows the main properties using a free scalar field in Minkowski space, gives a rigorous derivation for scalar fields in general curved spacetimes and evaluates it in specific cases. It also covers recent developments in conformal and integrable theories, the probability distribution of averaged energy measurements, the status in interacting models and various applications. It is a key reference on the limits of negative energy.
