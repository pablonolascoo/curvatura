The paper recalls that general relativity sets the speed of light as a limit, and that recent mathematical work by Eric Lentz, building on Alcubierre, suggests it could be exceeded. According to that work the required energies would be enormous and the physical mechanisms remain speculative. It proposes that matter-antimatter propulsion could bring a craft close to light speed and make acceleration toward warp speeds conceivable. It concludes that further theoretical work is needed with the Einstein and Maxwell equations for plasma solitons.
