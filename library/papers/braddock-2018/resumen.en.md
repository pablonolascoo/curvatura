This review article starts from the fact that routine exoplanet detection, such as the TRAPPIST-1 and K2-138 systems, will make it inevitable to shortlist worlds for exploration by probes or crewed ships. It recalls the physiological and psychological challenges of microgravity and focuses on solutions that would allow human travel lasting many years, such as artificial gravity. The title adds other deep space travel concepts, from warp drives and hibernation to world ships and cryogenics. It is an overview of ideas, with no calculations of its own described in the sources.
