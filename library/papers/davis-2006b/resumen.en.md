Davis explains that faster-than-light interstellar travel through traversable wormholes, warp drives or other spacetime modification schemes demands very special local geometries. Analysis with the general relativity field equations shows they require exotic matter, meaning matter with renormalized negative energy density, a term he says is misunderstood outside the relativity community. The work clarifies what negative energy is and where it occurs in nature, and reviews the experimental concepts proposed for generating it in the laboratory.
