The article presents two theories, intermediate factor theory and anti-factor theory, within a framework called Mule's Physics, built on five laws proposed by the author. According to the text, particles with certain dual-factor properties would allow interaction between spaces that otherwise do not couple, while others would cancel the effects of normal matter. Possible applications are claimed, such as superluminal travel, room-temperature superconductivity and communication with parallel universes. It is an unconventional proposal, with no comparison to established physics in the sources.
