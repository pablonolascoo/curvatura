The authors construct a kinematic analogue of superluminal travel in spacetimes warped by gravitation, in the form of super-phononic travel in the effective spacetimes of perfect nonrelativistic fluids. These warp-field spacetimes are easily generated by placing a solid object as an obstruction in a uniform flow. No condition on the positivity of energy needs to be violated, because the effective spacetime for phonons is governed by the Euler and continuity equations, not the Einstein equations.
