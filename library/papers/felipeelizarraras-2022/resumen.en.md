The work experimentally observes the stimulated analogue of Hawking radiation in a photonic crystal fiber, with a pulsed pump and a continuous wave probe. They propose and demonstrate a method to boost efficiency and probe the coherence of the effect using a double pump pulse with controlled delay. They show that the emitted radiation is the coherent, interfering sum of the Hawking signals from each pump pulse, and describe it with a simple Michelson type interference model that agrees very well with the data. At the interference maxima the signal grows by more than a factor of three, up to four under ideal conditions, and with m pulses it could grow as m squared.
