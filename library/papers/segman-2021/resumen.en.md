The article recalls that the energy density of the Alcubierre warp drive turns out to be negative everywhere. It takes up the shifting vector field proposed by Erik Lentz and argues that, together with a suitable spaceship geometry, it can give a positive energy density. It further suggests viewing the spaceship geometry as a mother wavelet function, with shifting, scaling and rotation operations that would add more positive energy density. This design would require a flexible fuselage able to stretch and rotate. It is a qualitative proposal with no figures in the sources.
