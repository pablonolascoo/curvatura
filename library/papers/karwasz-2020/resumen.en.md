The text discusses whether contemporary physics, with relativity and the uncertainty principle, removes causality, and argues that it does not. It claims there are no indications, classical or quantum, that physical laws are indeterministic at the ontological level, although in practice they are so at the epistemic level, because we cannot predict the detailed future. It adds that almost all physical principles, such as the arrow of time or energy conservation, could in theory be violated, but there is no experimental evidence that causality can be removed. It also includes didactic passages.
