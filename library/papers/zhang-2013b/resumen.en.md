The work studies the entanglement entropy associated with phonons generated through the Hawking mechanism in acoustic black holes of a Bose-Einstein condensate. It finds that the lowest energy allowed for the radiated phonons is a function of the space coordinate. Using this it computes the entanglement entropy, which has three parts: a constant leading term of value 1/6, a logarithmic correction and some series terms. The authors also discuss the convergence of those series terms.
