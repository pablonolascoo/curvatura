This Portuguese-language work starts from the fact that the constancy of the speed of light appears to be a barrier to interstellar travel, since reaching it would take infinite energy. It explains that general relativity allows this limit to be circumvented and arbitrarily high global speeds achieved without violating the local constancy of light. So-called warp metrics are a theoretical means for this, and the Alcubierre metric is the case that inspired the term. The study reviews special and general relativity and several works on solutions allowing superluminal speeds. It is an introductory review.
