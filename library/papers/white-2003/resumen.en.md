The article reconsiders the Alcubierre metric, in which a ship appears to vastly exceed the speed of light without doing so locally. It shows that the driving physical mechanism, at least mathematically, is not the expansion and contraction of the surrounding space through York time. What acts is a boost that multiplies the ship's initial velocity, comparable to watching a movie in fast-forward. The expansion and contraction of space would be a side effect, which can be seen as a kind of Doppler effect or stress on space. It offers a different reading of how the warp drive works.
