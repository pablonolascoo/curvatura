The work presents a laboratory experiment using a water tornado to model Keplerian flows, typical of astrophysical accretion discs. Two opposing jets create a hyperbolic free surface that acts as a gravitational potential. Tracer particles follow a Keplerian profile with Ω proportional to r to the power minus 3/2 and conserved areal speed. The measured effective Reynolds number is 2×10^5 and Stokes numbers range from 0.01 to 0.1. It is astrophysical fluid dynamics with no relation to propulsion.
