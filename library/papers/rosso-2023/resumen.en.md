The authors construct localized states in a ball or half-space of a conformal field theory in Minkowski space that are thermal with respect to the local modular flow. They compute their energy density at arbitrary temperature in several theories and find values where it is negative and diverges at the boundary. Even so, the energy measured by an observer is consistent with the bounds known in the literature. In holographic theories these states correspond to hyperbolic black holes in anti-de Sitter space, where the negative energy equals the black hole's negative mass. They also extend a proof of the holographic entanglement formula to half-spaces.
