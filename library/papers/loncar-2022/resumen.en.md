The work introduces a new approach to realizing negative inertia and the concept of negative-inertia converters, for translational and rotational motion. The devices move a loading inertia hidden inside their housing in sync with the housing's displacement, so they exhibit negative mass and negative moment of inertia. They resemble negative-impedance converters, including their proneness to instability. The authors develop and simulate an equivalent circuit model, in lossless and lossy settings, and find that friction causes the instability. The stability condition requires that the effective inertia of the system remain positive.
