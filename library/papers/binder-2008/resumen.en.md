The authors propose a flat holographic universe model in which compressing the low-dimensional quantum memory expands the cosmic image and vice versa, with an intermediate inversion scale. Assigning 4D, 2D and 3D topologies to the cosmic image, the baryonic memory and the inversion scale, the critical density equals one memory unit. From this they obtain a 96% dark matter deficit and a theoretical Hubble parameter of 73.2 km/s/Mpc. For propulsion, they propose fractional linear transforms that map quantum compression into cosmic expansion; the anisotropic version resembles the Alcubierre mechanism, with expansion behind and compression ahead of the ship.
