The text describes the physical features of traversable wormholes, or stargates, derived from classical general relativity, and how they might be created. It summarises the engineering parameters that characterise them to show the technical challenges of making one in the laboratory. It then proposes the hypothesis that the UFO phenomenon would be a manifestation of this technology, a hypothesis with no support in the sources. It also presents an alternative theory of gravity that would allow faster-than-light motion, parallel to wormholes and warp drives.
