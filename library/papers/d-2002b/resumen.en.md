Negative energy objects usually cause instabilities and other troubling behavior; in particular, negative energy fluxes break the classical area theorem for black hole horizons and can violate the second law of thermodynamics. The authors analyze the negative energy objects of string theory, whose special boundary conditions remove perturbative instabilities. They show these objects have further features that let them avoid contradicting the second law: they identify one mechanism that applies to most orientifold planes and different ones for the O8 plane and the AdS soliton.
