The article presents warp drives, born in science fiction and now a topic in theoretical physics. It explains that the geometry Alcubierre proposed in 1994 is considered unphysical because it violates all energy conditions and requires large amounts of exotic matter. It discusses the energy condition violations and the energy requirements obtained from quantum inequalities, and the definition of superluminal travel. It also studies the horizon problem and the possibility of closed timelike curves in this spacetime, and presents recent progress. It concludes that, though unlikely as technology, they are useful thought experiments that test the limits of current theories.
