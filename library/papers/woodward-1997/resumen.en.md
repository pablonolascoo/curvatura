The paper briefly reviews the scientific reasons for trying to make traversable wormholes and examines methods for doing so with a Machian transient mass fluctuation. It mentions several problems that might arise, which the author says may be only engineering difficulties. It also examines the use of quantum inequalities to constrain the negative mass-energy needed to form wormholes, and argues that these inequalities do not prohibit the creation of artificial concentrations of negative mass-energy.
