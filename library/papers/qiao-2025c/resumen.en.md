The article proposes to manipulate the spacetime metric with gauge-transformed optical soliton beams that generate gravitational solitons, so that a toroidal time machine reaches apparent superluminal speeds from 2c to 10^7 c and time regressions of, for example, 32.8 years. It shows that the Lagrangian of two optical solitons can be turned into a gravitational soliton one through an electromagnetic-gravitational coupling. It uses eight tangentially emitted beams and studies the effect of polarisation, energy density and number of pairs. The authors acknowledge that interstellar travel and temporal paradoxes need further study.
