The paper proposes a cylindrically symmetric spacetime describing pure radiation that satisfies the energy conditions. By solving the geodesic equations, it shows the spacetime admits circular closed timelike geodesics. The spacetime is regular, with no curvature singularities, and its null geodesic congruences are non-diverging and shear-free. A linear perturbation analysis finds those geodesics to be stable, and a modified version produces closed timelike curves after a certain instant, that is, a time machine. It is of interest because closed curves are obtained without exotic matter.
