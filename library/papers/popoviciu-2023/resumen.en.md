This chapter starts from the fact that the solar system is a tiny fraction of the galaxy and that many stars host planets, starting with Alpha Centauri. The first problem for expanding across the universe is arriving in reasonable times, which the author says could only be done by faster-than-light travel that bypasses normal spacetime, although research is at its beginnings. Meanwhile thousands of exoplanets have been found, some easy to terraform and settle and others colonizable with planetary engineering. It is a popular and forward-looking text.
