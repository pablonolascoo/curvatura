The work analyses the dynamics of a gas bubble inflating in a compressible, viscous fluid whose flow follows a Hubble-like field, proportional to the radial coordinate. It derives the equation of motion of the bubble wall and computes the speed of sound on it, with numerical solutions and approximate analytical ones in families of conics. It proposes a two-dimensional universe on the wall whose points recede according to Hubble's law and discusses cosmological fine-tuning problems. It points to an analogy between surface tension and the cosmological constant.
