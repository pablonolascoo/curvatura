The authors apply quantum inequality type restrictions to Alcubierre's warp drive metric, on a scale where a local region of spacetime can be considered flat. These inequalities limit the magnitude and extent of the negative energy needed to form the metric. From them they derive limits on the warp bubble parameters: the wall thickness would be only a few hundred Planck lengths. They then show that the total integrated energy density needed to maintain a metric with such thin walls is physically unattainable, hence the title about its unphysical nature.
