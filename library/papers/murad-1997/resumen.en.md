The paper discusses a candidate faster-than-light drive that would benefit from growing electromagnetic effects when the ship moves at or above light speed. It presents an alternative derivation of Maxwell's equations with magnetic and electric charges using an "inverse" Lorentz gauge condition and pseudoanalytic functions. It examines the limits of using general instead of special relativity and outlines the use of ball lightning. Finally it transforms the trajectory equations into a second-order equation with a light-speed "switch".
