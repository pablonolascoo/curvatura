The work studies the Casimir effect for a massive scalar field with non-conformal coupling in a wormhole whose throat is surrounded by a spherical shell. Using zeta regularisation it computes the zero-point energy of the field. It finds that, depending on the coupling, the field mass and the throat radius, the Casimir force can be attractive, repulsive or even zero. It is relevant because vacuum fluctuations are a possible source of negative energy to hold wormholes open.
