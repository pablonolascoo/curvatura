The work clears up an apparent contradiction about traversable wormholes in two modified gravity theories: Brans-Dicke and f(R). In Brans-Dicke, wormholes had been found only in a narrow range of the parameter omega and not for omega equal to zero, whereas in f(R), which is that limit, their existence was shown. After careful analysis, the authors conclude that the compared studies dealt with wormholes having different shape and redshift functions, so the conclusions differ without conflicting. They spell out the starting conditions of each solution.
