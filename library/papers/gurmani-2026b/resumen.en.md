The authors construct a static, spherical, traversable wormhole in Finsler-Randers gravity, where the anisotropy of spacetime is encoded by a weak timelike vector field. They take a Morris-Thorne wormhole with no tidal forces and a fixed shape function, and derive closed-form expressions for the energy density and the pressures. They show that the amount of matter violating the null energy condition decreases as the anisotropy grows, and that the violating region shrinks to a neighbourhood of the throat. They also derive a TOV-type equilibrium equation with an extra geometric force and study the throat and tidal forces.
