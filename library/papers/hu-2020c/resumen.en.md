This chapter computes the noise kernel of the stress-energy tensor in de Sitter spacetime, for a de Sitter-invariant vacuum. That geometry approximates most inflationary models well, so its fluctuations have been widely studied. After reviewing the geometric properties and invariant bitensors, the authors write the noise kernel for spacelike separated points and give explicit expressions for a free minimally coupled scalar field. They obtain closed results in elementary functions for small masses, zero mass and large separations, and find a discontinuity in the massless limit. They discuss the implications for quantum metric fluctuations.
