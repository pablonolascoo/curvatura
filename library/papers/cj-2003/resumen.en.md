The work formulates a notion of quantum weak energy inequality for general dynamical systems on static backgrounds and links it to thermodynamics. It shows that if a class of states obeying such an inequality exists, then passive states exist and the second law holds. The model system is the free scalar field on a static spacetime, using a related algebra that is carefully constructed. It matters because these inequalities limit how much negative energy can occur, which constrains wormholes and warp bubbles.
