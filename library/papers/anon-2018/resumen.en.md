This work uses the generalized second law of thermodynamics to prove a singularity theorem, extending the notion of a trapped surface to quantum situations. It concludes that spacetime is incomplete inside black holes, as in Penrose's original theorem. In asymptotically flat spacetime, the same law would rule out traversable wormholes, negative masses, other faster-than-light travel and closed timelike curves. The author argues that, since the semiclassical approximation is used only where curvature is low, the results may hold in full quantum gravity.
