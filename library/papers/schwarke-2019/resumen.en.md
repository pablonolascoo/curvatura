This chapter, in German, is a cultural study of science fiction. It interprets the spore drive in Star Trek: Discovery as a reflection of current debates in ecological and technology ethics. It sees it as a technical approach to omnipresence, once reserved for the divine, which shows the ambivalence of realising utopias: behind omnipresence there was always a wish for power. When it is achieved and the boundaries between technology, nature and mind dissolve in the mycelium, problems can no longer be externalised. It is not a physics work.
