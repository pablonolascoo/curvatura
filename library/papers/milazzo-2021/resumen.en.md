This thesis looks for optimal measurement strategies to estimate and characterize quantum states and channels, with emphasis on entanglement correlations, which is needed to certify that quantum processors really work in a quantum way. It addresses entanglement certification in a multi-qubit system with partial information, using an algorithm based on the truncated moment sequence problem that identifies the most efficient measurement sequence. It also studies the separability of quantum channels through the Choi matrix, where the same framework gives a unified necessary and sufficient condition. It is quantum information, unrelated to propulsion.
