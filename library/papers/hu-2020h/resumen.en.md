This chapter presents the Schwinger-DeWitt effective action in the in-out formalism, suited to computing transition amplitudes, and applies it to backreaction problems in dynamical spacetimes. After reviewing quantum fields in curved space, it builds the effective action of an interacting field and uses it to study particle creation in the Friedmann-Lemaitre-Robertson-Walker universe, showing how dimensional regularisation is implemented. It then develops a derivative expansion for slowly varying backgrounds and ends with the renormalisation group equations in Phi-4 theory.
