The paper briefly reviews the idea of a reactionless, propellant-free drive and analytically derives one based on a specific application of the Abraham-Lorentz force. It presents simulated data with the main characteristics and a benchtop model of the theoretical device. The authors note that current schemes emitting ions, particles or light give minuscule thrust and that no reactionless drive has been shown to work in practice. They conclude that their drive is mathematically feasible, although the source offers no independent experimental verification. It is a speculative propellantless propulsion proposal.
