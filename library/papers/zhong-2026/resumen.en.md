The article gathers three negative results from ninety years of wormhole physics: the Einstein-Rosen bridge is untraversable because its throat pinches off faster than any timelike worldline can cross; traversable wormholes require exotic matter violating the null energy condition, with quantum inequalities strongly limiting the available negative energy; and no search has produced candidates. It proposes a common explanation: the second asymptotic region of Schwarzschild would be the geometric registration of a substrate level, not another copy of spacetime, and material systems could not cross into it. It is a speculative interpretation.
