The article outlines a generic framework for foresight processes, based on earlier work by Mintzberg, Horton and Slaughter. It was developed while introducing foresight into the formal strategic planning of a public university in Australia. The framework distinguishes several phases, from the initial gathering of information to the production of outputs that feed strategy development and planning. It also serves as a diagnostic tool for how foresight and strategy are done and as a design aid for customized projects. The author adds reflections from two and a half years as an organization-based foresight practitioner. It does not deal with physics.
