We construct quantum reference frames (QRFs) in quantum field theory, using localized modes of a scalar field as frames for its shift symmetry. We import tools from relativistic quantum information to explicitly compute entropies and correlation measures of relational observables, investigating their frame dependence and the new features arising from the field-theoretic character of the system. Unlike QRFs for systems with finitely many degrees of freedom, localized field-theoretic QRFs are necessarily mixed in physically relevant states and generically correlated with the subsystems they describe. These aspects of QRFs for fields affect the properties of relational observables in a subtle way. Furthermore, we put forward a proposal for testing these predictions with existing quantum simulators based on ultracold atoms and quantum fluids, thus connecting QRF physics with analogue gravity platforms.
