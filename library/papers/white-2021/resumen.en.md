While analyzing for a DARPA funded project the structure of the energy density in a Casimir cavity under the dynamic vacuum model, a microstructure was found that predicts a negative energy density distribution closely matching the requirements of the Alcubierre metric. The simplest geometry is a parallel plate cavity with pillars along the midplane. Using a numerical worldline technique, the calculation gave results qualitatively similar to the two dimensional representation of the Alcubierre requirements. A toy model, with a 1 micron sphere in a 4 micron cylinder, showed a similar three dimensional correlation, and suggests chip scale experiments to measure a tiny warp bubble.
