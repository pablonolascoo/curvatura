This paper, presented at a NASA workshop on breakthrough propulsion physics, notes that Alcubierre's effective superluminal motion has been shown to be physically unattainable by Pfenning and Ford. As an alternative, it presents the hypothesis that the inertia and gravitation of matter originate in electromagnetic interactions between the zero-point field and the quarks and electrons making up atoms. A new covariant derivation of the link between the zero-point field and inertia is given, yielding the relativistic equation of motion from Maxwell's equations. The idea would open the door to radically new propulsion, though it remains a hypothesis.
