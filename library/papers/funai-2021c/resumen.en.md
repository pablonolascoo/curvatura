This thesis studies light-matter interactions from three angles. First, it develops a feasible procedure for generating exotic energy distributions, showing that quantum energy teleportation can create local negative energy densities using stationary qubits. The total negative energy grows as the inverse cube of the well width, accompanied by matching positive peaks, and this scaling saturates the quantum interest conjecture. The other two strands examine the validity of the dipole approximation with a quantum electromagnetic field and the causal consequences of common approximations in relativistic quantum fields.
