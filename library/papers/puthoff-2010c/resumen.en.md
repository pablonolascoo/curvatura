The article makes the case for vacuum engineering as a basis for advanced propulsion. It recalls that the vacuum is an energetic and structured medium: according to quantum theory it hosts particle and field fluctuations, and according to general relativity it is the seat of a metric encoding the distribution of matter and energy. From this it proposes altering its properties to obtain new phenomena and, specifically, modifying the spacetime metric to drive spacecraft. The sources give the conceptual framing, without quantitative results.
