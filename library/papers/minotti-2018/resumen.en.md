The authors start from the fact that the scalar-tensor theory of Mbelek and Lachièze-Rey might explain the forces reported in asymmetric microwave cavities. They show that its revised version predicts a much simpler way of producing thrust by electrostatic means. The equations indicate a constant force on a spherical capacitor with asymmetric mass distribution kept at constant voltage. They also propose a Cavendish-like experiment with the capacitor in place of the large mass, as a way to test this gravity theory.
