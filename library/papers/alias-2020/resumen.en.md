The article reviews mathematically how Alcubierre's warp propulsion is derived within general relativity, in contrast to wormholes, which break spacetime topology. It starts from building the metric and arrives at the expression for the energy density that governs the contraction and expansion of spacetime around the vehicle. The parameters describing the warp bubble are laid out. It is an expository, derivation-style work, useful as an introduction to the formalism of the warp drive.
