The work discusses the physics of the quantum stress tensor operator. Its expectation values are well understood, but the fluctuations around the mean are less so. It is shown that the correlation function decomposes into three parts: one finite and state dependent, one infinite in the coincidence limit and state independent, and a cross term both state dependent and infinite. The tensor's fluctuations induce fluctuations of the geometry, and the singular terms seem to yield a stochastic spacetime. Whether these are observable remains an open question.
