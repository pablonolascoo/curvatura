The authors show that the Chung-Freese ansatz describes a new braneworld that, although superluminal, does not suffer from the pathologies of other faster than light methods. They also show that our universe would be a trapped surface inside a universe with large extra dimensions, and present two trapping mechanisms that keep Standard Model fields inside the brane. For this model they develop a formalism similar to Morris domain walls and present our universe as a low energy state in a potential well. Finally, they point out that the Israel condition can also trap the fields.
