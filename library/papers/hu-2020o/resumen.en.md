This chapter is an overview of a book on semiclassical and stochastic gravity. It explains how three levels relate: quantum field theory in curved spacetime, semiclassical gravity and stochastic gravity, and lays out the central themes of backreaction and fluctuations, the mathematical tools and their applications to early universe cosmology and black holes. It clarifies that the Newton-Schrödinger equation does not belong to semiclassical gravity. It is a foundational framework for quantum negative energy and quantum back-reaction on geometry.
