The authors start from a vacuum filled with virtual wormholes. An external field changes their distribution and could, in principle, allow a coherent structure that works as an actual wormhole or as an Alcubierre bubble. They establish an explicit functional relation between the external field and the distribution of virtual wormholes, which opens a way to study their artificial creation in a laboratory. They show that weak, quasi homogeneous fields suppress the density of wormholes and cannot be used, and they discuss a threshold in field intensity above which wormhole type structures could form.
