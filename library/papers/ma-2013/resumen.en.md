The authors derive the entanglement entropy of an acoustic black hole in a Bose-Einstein condensate, associated with the phonons generated by the Hawking mechanism in a sonic hole. Taking the condensate's dispersion relation into account, they recalculate the entropy with statistical methods in two limits. They find it is still proportional to the area of the event horizon, but with a coefficient that depends on the infrared cutoff. It is an analogue gravity result linking horizon thermodynamics with laboratory systems.
