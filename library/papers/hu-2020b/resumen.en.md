This chapter applies stochastic gravity by deriving the Einstein-Langevin equation for metric perturbations on a Minkowski background. It solves it for the linearized Einstein tensor and computes the two-point correlation functions. The results show that gravitational fluctuations are negligible above the Planck length and strongly suppressed at smaller scales, which links to the 1/N expansion of quantum gravity. It also studies the stability of Minkowski spacetime in semiclassical gravity and deals with runaway solutions.
