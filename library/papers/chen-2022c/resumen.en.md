This is the closing chapter of a Springer thesis on ultracold quantum gases. It gathers the main work done between 2016 and 2021, part of which has already appeared in journal articles and part of which is still to come. It also outlines ongoing and near term projects that can be carried out with the group's compact and versatile quantum gas apparatus, and gives an outlook for the research. It reports no new numerical results, and its link to warp propulsion is remote.
