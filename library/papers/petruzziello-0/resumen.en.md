The work introduces a decoherence process caused by quantum gravity effects, as a possible mechanism for the quantum-to-classical transition. It assumes a foamy quantum spacetime with a fluctuating minimal length coinciding on average with the Planck scale. With deformed canonical commutation relations and a fluctuating deformation parameter, it derives a Lindblad master equation that gives localization in energy space and decoherence times consistent with available evidence. The decoherence rate is extremal, minimal below the Planck scale and maximal in the mesoscopic regime. It discusses tests in cavity optomechanics with ultracold molecular oscillators.
