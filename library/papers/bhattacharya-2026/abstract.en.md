We propose a novel way to achieve the regularization of the JNW globally naked singularity sourced by the canonical Einstein minimally coupled scalar field (EMS). The key idea is to complexify (Wick rotate) the JNW parameters, which yields a regular metric that surprisingly represents a twice asymptotically flat regular traversable wormhole, famously known as the Ellis class III wormhole sourced by the ghost EMS. We begin by arguing that the JNW solution belongs to a hierarchy of actions describing different gravity theories, which indicates that a regularized version of JNW automatically adds corresponding regular versions to the hierarchy of gravity theories.
