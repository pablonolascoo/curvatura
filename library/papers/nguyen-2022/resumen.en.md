This preprint from a small R&D company presents a set of technological developments that, according to its authors, would enable human cosmic flight. It lists areas such as topology and transformation of complex spacetime, a black hole-Big Bang hybrid model, superluminal micro-gravitational hyper-drives, superluminal navigation and quantum communication, wireless power transfer and the economics of planetary settlement. The sources give only the list of topics, with no verifiable methods or results, and it is a speculative proposal.
