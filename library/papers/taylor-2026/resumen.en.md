The work proposes a cosmological framework in which spacetime curvature, the arrow of time and cosmic acceleration arise from a single scalar entropy field, defined as the logarithm of the local quantum partition function. Its first derivatives encode energy flow and temporal direction, and its second derivatives generate curvature. This reproduces features attributed to dark energy and dark matter, and explains without fine-tuning the huge gap between zero-point energy and the cosmological constant. Regions of negative entropic curvature would correspond to the conditions for warp bubbles and wormholes.
