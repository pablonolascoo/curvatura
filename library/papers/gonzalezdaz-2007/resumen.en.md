The letter considers a warp drive spacetime in which the ship can only travel faster than light. Restricting to the two dimensional case, the author finds that if the warp drive sits in an accelerating universe, the bubble size grows comovingly with the expansion of the universe it is immersed in. He also shows that the ship's apparent velocity increases steadily with time as the ship accretes phantom energy. This links warp drives with dark energy and cosmic acceleration.
