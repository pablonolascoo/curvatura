The study uses photon trajectories, that is null geodesics, as rays in a raycasting system to visualize spacetime warping such as gravitational lensing. Geometry enters as a metric tensor or isotropic line element, so the program is modular and accepts different theoretical spacetimes. The authors test it on a Schwarzschild black hole and on the Alcubierre warp drive. They report that the results show accuracy and versatility in rendering complex geometries, and they propose future work using gravitational wave data and improving computational efficiency.
