The article proposes a geometric reinterpretation of pressure in inviscid potential flows, derived from a single variational principle over the fluid volume and the fluid-solid interface. The bulk action enforces incompressibility with a Lagrange multiplier identified with pressure, and the curvature-dependent boundary action enforces impermeability and yields a surface stress tensor. It reproduces the classical wall-pressure distribution and canonical solutions for cylinders and airfoils via the Joukowski mapping. It is fluid mechanics, unrelated to exotic propulsion.
