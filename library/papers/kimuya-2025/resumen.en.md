This preprint presents an experimental electric circuit setup with time-varying resistance and bidirectional chaotic currents, with which the author claims to challenge the principle of energy conservation. A short parallel connection would prevent backflow and redirect short-circuit currents, and an Arduino-based acquisition system records voltages and currents in several phases. The author claims the measured power exceeds the prediction of the standard Ohm's law and that the current stabilizes despite very low resistances. It is an extraordinary claim that is not validated, and its link to propulsion is remote.
