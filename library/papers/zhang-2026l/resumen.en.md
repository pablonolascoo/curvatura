The article studies the cosmological energy conditions in f(T, B, L_m) gravity, a teleparallel extension of general relativity that describes gravity by torsion rather than curvature. In this theory the torsion scalar and the boundary term couple nonminimally to the matter Lagrangian, adding geometric contributions to the effective energy-momentum tensor. The resulting energy conditions are general and reduce to those of other theories such as f(T,B), f(T,L_m) or f(R). They serve to check the physical viability of the theory.
