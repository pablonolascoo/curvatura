We construct an exactly solvable hierarchy of static, spherically symmetric, wormholes with arbitrary finite throat multiplicity. A factorized prescription for the derivative of the areal radius places every stationary sphere at a prescribed proper distance while retaining elementary integrability and asymptotic flatness. The order-$N$ geometry contains exactly $N+1$ throats and $N$ anti-throats, arranged as an alternating chain of necks and finite interior cavities. Independently of this particular hierarchy, the Einstein equations imply three universal ultrastatic results. Every nondegenerate throat violates the radial null energy condition, whereas every nondegenerate anti-throat satisfies the null, weak, dominant, and strong energy conditions, with the strong-energy sum condition saturated. The same local combination $ρ+p_\ell$ fixes the radial stability of the associated circular null orbit through $V_{\mathrm {null}}''=8πL^2(ρ+p_\ell)/\mathcal R^2$; throats are therefore unstable light rings and anti-throats are stable ones. Finally, every finite cell between consecutive stationary spheres, each exterior cell, and the complete two-ended radial null geodesic carry strictly negative integrated radial null energy. The resulting spacetimes are exact multi-cavity optical chains whose cavity centers are locally nonexotic even though no cell can eliminate averaged radial exoticity. The first nontrivial member interpolates continuously from the Ellis-Bronnikov wormhole through a quartically degenerate throat to a double-throat geometry and admits closed expressions for its source, null average, branchwise Morris-Thorne form, embedding criterion, and traversability scales. Its vanishing Arnowitt-Deser-Misner mass follows from the specific radial falloff of the hierarchy, rather than from ultrastaticity alone.
