The author derives a general structure of Lorentz transformations, the GLT model, which includes the Lorentz-Einstein and Galilean transformations as particular cases. Since its free parameters are identified in a gravitational field, he states it could be used in special and general relativity and would open the way to unifying both and the electromagnetic and gravitational fields. If the model is correct, new phenomena would appear, such as length and time neutrality, occurring when the velocity equals the free-fall velocity. The text mentions travel time reduction.
