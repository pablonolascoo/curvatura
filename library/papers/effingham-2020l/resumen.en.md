This philosophy chapter asks what is rational to do in a time travel case where a dying future self warns of a nuclear war, assuming the past cannot be changed. It shows a consistent scenario in which the protagonist is led to believe in a war that never happened and is sent back, so that everyone else lives. It treats this as a Newcomb-type case and argues that both one-boxers and two-boxers should use those excessive means. An appendix argues that, with a perfect predictor, the two-boxer should instead choose one box.
