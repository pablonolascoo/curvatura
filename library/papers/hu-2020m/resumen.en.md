This chapter studies the backreaction problem in early universe cosmology, that is, finding solutions to the semiclassical Einstein equation, the heart of semiclassical gravity. It presents four groups of problems at the Planck and grand unification scales: effects of the trace anomaly, which could help avoid the singularity and give Starobinsky inflation; effects of particle creation on singularities and horizons; post-inflationary preheating with a Phi-4 model; and stochastic inflation. It also examines, in quantum cosmology, whether the minisuperspace approximation is valid.
