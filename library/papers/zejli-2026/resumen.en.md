The article proposes a subluminal analogue of the Alcubierre metric to engineer the sign structure of the stress-energy tensor using Casimir cavities and a magnetised plasma. Micro and nanostructured cavities provide a localised negative-energy signature, and the plasma, at about 0.1c, a measurable positive background. A modified Alcubierre metric with a double hyperbolic tangent profile is used, regular and horizon-free, only kinematically. The authors do not claim a self-consistent Einstein-Maxwell solution. They outline a tabletop testbed and a Z-pinch one, to validate methods rather than generate measurable curvature.
