The article presents and develops several nonlinear energy conditions suited to the semiclassical regime, in particular the flux energy condition and two new ones: trace-of-square and determinant. It shows that these conditions behave much better than the classical linear energy conditions in the presence of semiclassical quantum effects. Moreover, their quantum extensions seem to be widely satisfied on entering the quantum realm, whereas analogous extensions of the linear conditions are generally not useful. It matters because classical conditions are easily violated in wormholes and warp drives.
