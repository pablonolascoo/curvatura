The duality symmetry of Maxwell's equations in vacuum implies conservation of the circular polarization of classical electromagnetic waves, and in quantum field theory it corresponds to the difference between right and left handed photons. The work shows this Noether symmetry also exists in empty cylindrical waveguides with duality preserving boundary conditions, and quantizes the field in that setting. For observers in relativistic helical motion, the expectation value of that charge is no longer conserved, and frame dragging produces a spectral asymmetry between modes of the two helicities. It proposes a proof of concept on analogue gravity platforms.
