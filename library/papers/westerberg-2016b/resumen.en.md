The authors theoretically study how to create artificial spacetime geometries for light using intense laser pulses that modify an optical medium, with metrics of the form ds2 = c2dt2 - eta(t)2dx2. With a generalised Hopfield model they estimate how many photons the time-dependent spacetime generates. In feasible conditions the photon pairs would be very few, but periodic modulation would give resonance as in the dynamical Casimir effect and much larger production. That metric resembles a gravitational wave, so a periodic wave could amplify photons.
