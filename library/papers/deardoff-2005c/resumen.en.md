The paper argues that current physics ideas, such as inflation with anthropic reasoning, the parallel universes of string theory and traversable wormholes, make the solution that we are alone to Fermi's paradox less credible. It therefore re-examines the assumption that extraterrestrials do not visit Earth and proposes looking for evidence in high-quality UFO reports. It is a speculative argument rather than a calculation, linked to interstellar travel and wormholes.
