The author studies ADM-compatible warp geometries in which the shift vector is the gradient of a scalar potential subject to a parabolic, diffusion-like relation. He derives the extrinsic curvature and the Hamiltonian energy density in terms of the potential's Hessian, analyzes the weak energy condition for an electromagnetic and plasma source, and gives a numerical illustration. Because the parabolic ansatz implies instantaneous influence, he introduces a telegraph-type hyperbolic regularization with finite characteristic speeds. He thus discusses the "Catch-22" causality objection.
