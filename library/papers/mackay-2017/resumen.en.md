The chapter explains that, from a noncovariant perspective, the electromagnetic properties of vacuous curved spacetime are equivalent to those of a fictitious medium in flat space, the Tamm medium, which is bianisotropic, nonreciprocal and nonhomogeneous. This allows tabletop study of curved spacetime scenarios that are hard to investigate directly. It proposes realizing Tamm media as homogenized composites, using the Bruggeman formalism in an inverse sense to estimate constitutive parameters, particle shapes and volume fractions.
