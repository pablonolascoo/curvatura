The authors derive the acoustic metric and effective action using a shift transformation and the functional integral method; this metric describes fluctuations of phonon fields in a quantum fluid. By coupling the phonon fields to two kinds of two-dimensional gravity, the Jackiw-Teitelboim and Almheiri-Polchinski models, they compute the effective phonon mass and the Hawking temperature analytically. They also analyse how the fluctuation strength and the deformation parameter affect them. It is a work on acoustic black holes in analogue gravity.
