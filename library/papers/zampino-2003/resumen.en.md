The paper explores applying risk assessment to NASA's frontier Breakthrough Propulsion Physics project, whose goals are to reduce or eliminate propellant mass, achieve travel near or beyond the speed of light and find new modes of onboard energy conversion. Since the project is at the emerging-science stage with no conceptual designs yet, the authors still propose thinking about the risks of the various research paths and point out areas where it would apply. It is a management work, not a physics one.
