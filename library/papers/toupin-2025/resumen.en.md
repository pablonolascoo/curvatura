The work studies whether gravity-like phenomena such as bending, time delay, shifts and orbits can arise as media analogues from deterministic scalar-field propagation, without mass or curvature. They evolve a real scalar field with a spatially varying transport tensor and damping field, using thirteen simulations on 256 by 256 grids with confirmations at 512 by 512. Bending scales with the gradient of the tensor and flips sign when it is reversed; delay grows with the integral of the damping. These are operational analogues of gravity.
