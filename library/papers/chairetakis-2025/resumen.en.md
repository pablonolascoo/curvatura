The preprint proposes a theoretical framework for extratemporal propulsion, called the Aeternum Drive, based on a post-relativistic extension of informational spacetime. The mechanism uses an emergent scalar field representing an entropic-temporal deformation. The authors state that relativistic limits are preserved and that higher-order corrections in time topology appear, allowing causally safe transitions. They combine generalised geometrodynamics, nonlocal entanglement and informational curvature, and suggest testable effects and vacuum energy extraction. It is a speculative proposal with no validation in the source.
