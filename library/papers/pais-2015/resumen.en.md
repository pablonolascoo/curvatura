The paper applies the author's conditional replacement technique to special relativity. It observes that under certain physical conditions the singularity of the gamma factor as a spacecraft approaches the speed of light is no longer present. This would involve instantly removing energy-mass from the system when the craft reaches v = c/2. Under such conditions, according to the author, faster-than-light propulsion would be feasible, not violating special relativity but building on it. It is an untested theoretical proposal, with no numerical results in the sources.
