Fewster and Mistry gave an explicit, non-optimal quantum weak energy inequality that bounds the energy density of Dirac fields in Minkowski spacetime. This work adapts their argument to the case of flat two dimensional spacetime. The non-optimal bound obtained is of the same order of magnitude, in the zero mass limit, as Vollick's optimal bound. It helps limit how much negative energy Dirac fields can carry, which is useful for judging the viability of exotic metrics.
