The paper starts from the fact that spacetime can be somewhat distorted by intense electromagnetic effects, but that ordinary electromagnetic fields do not couple to gravitational ones because they are of a different nature. It argues that if electromagnetic radiation could be given an essence and form similar to gravitation, it might couple with it for propulsion. It describes several ways of creating specially conditioned electromagnetic radiation and several ways its fields might interact with those of gravity or with the vacuum's zero-point state. It is a conceptual inertia-reduction proposal, with no verified results in the source.
