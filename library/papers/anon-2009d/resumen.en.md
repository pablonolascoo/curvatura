This chapter asks whether humanity will be able to overcome interstellar distances or remain confined to the solar system and nearby stars. It describes the scale of the Milky Way, about a hundred thousand light-years across, and notes that neighbouring stars lie around ten light-years away, so even at light speed a round trip takes up to about twenty years. It is a popular introduction to the future of galactic exploration, following the pessimistic conclusions of the previous chapter, and frames the motivation for advanced propulsion.
