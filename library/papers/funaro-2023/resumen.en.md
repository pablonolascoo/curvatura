The author argues that electromagnetic waves, in vacuum or in matter, dynamically alter the spacetime metric, which can be solved explicitly in some cases with Einstein's equation. In his framework, the wave's passage distorts the molecular lattice of a dielectric, and if the wave or the body is asymmetric a net force could arise. This motivated a laboratory experiment with a ring of particular geometry that, according to the text, produced a directional thrust in apparent violation of action and reaction. The approach could be a starting point for propellantless propulsion, though the sources give no figures.
