This thesis studies quantum light-matter interactions from three angles: how to use them to create exotic energy distributions, how far the classical approximation holds, and what causal consequences certain relativistic approximations have. Its first aim is an operational procedure to generate negative energy density without accelerated mirrors or nonlinear crystals, using a quantum energy teleportation protocol with stationary qubits. The effect of detector smearing is also discussed. It matters for negative energy, a requirement of warp bubbles.
