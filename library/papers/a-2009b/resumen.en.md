This review examines the nature of time and causality within special and general relativity. It covers closed timelike curves in general relativity and the paradoxes they bring, the question of the direction of time's flow and, more briefly, the problem of time in quantum gravity. It is a conceptual survey rather than a new calculation. It matters for exotic propulsion because wormholes and superluminal bubbles can produce closed timelike curves, which is the source of the causality objections to them.
