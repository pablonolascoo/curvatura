A metaphysical, speculative text arguing that all things are connected. It starts from how matter would arise from total void, complementary or dark matter and the incompleteness of Einstein's relativity. It mentions multi-spacetime universes, a jump drive between them and a warp drive for space travel. It ties these to DNA, Ezekiel's dry bones prophecy and consciousness as the collection of cords of consciousness. The sources show no calculations or empirical verification.
