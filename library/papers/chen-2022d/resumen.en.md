This introductory thesis chapter presents a fully controllable quantum simulator for studying out-of-equilibrium dynamics in atomic quantum gases. The usual difficulty is that mass transport and the spreading of quantum correlations appear mixed together. The approach removes density inhomogeneity and uses local control of the chemical potential and interactions, so that collective dynamics can be started and probed with or without mass flow. It has no direct link to propulsion; it is ultracold gas physics.
