The article asks what would be wrong with replacing Einstein's light postulate with a sound postulate, whereby the speed of sound is independent of the source's speed. It reviews the historical reasons for light's special role and the conditions under which a sonic relativity would be empirically justified. It then draws philosophical consequences for global versus subsystem symmetries, dynamical versus geometrical approaches to spacetime, preferred frames in quantum gravity and spacetime functionalism. It is a conceptual work with no direct link to propulsion.
