The work studies the physical properties of static, spherically symmetric Morris-Thorne traversable wormholes in modified f(R,T) gravity. Analysing the energy conditions, it finds a throat region with positive energy density but a violated radial null energy condition, implying the throat is held by radial tension. The exoticity is directional and is attributed to the curvature-matter coupling and the higher-order curvature term. The volume integral quantifier is nearly null, so there is no large reservoir of exotic matter. A stability analysis is added.
