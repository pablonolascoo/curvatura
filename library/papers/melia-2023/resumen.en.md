The paper argues that the "numen" scalar field, derived from the zero active mass condition in general relativity, satisfies all the energy conditions in the early universe. It recalls that these conditions are used to prove theorems such as the singularity theorems, but appear to be violated in scenarios like inflation and late-time acceleration. Abandoning them would open the door to traversable wormholes and time loops. Since numen satisfies them all and inflation violates at least one, the author sees extra justification for favouring the former.
