This chapter derives the linearized Riemann tensor correlator on a de Sitter background, including one-loop corrections from conformal fields. The correlation function is gauge-invariant when matter loops are included (without graviton loops, as in the 1/N expansion), is compatible with de Sitter invariance and fully characterizes the local geometry. It is computed by differentiating the metric correlator from the previous chapter and written in manifestly invariant form. Correlators for the Weyl and Ricci tensors and their mixture are also given, with their subhorizon and superhorizon behavior, and the result is extended to general conformal theories and to Minkowski space.
