The text addresses the problem of determinism in general relativity, which appears even if spacetime is globally hyperbolic. The strong cosmic censorship hypothesis amounts to saying that general relativity is a predictive theory, a central issue of the classical theory. The author sketches the proof of inextendibility of the maximal globally hyperbolic development for Electrogowdy spacetimes. It is relevant to the limits of causality and prediction in exotic geometries.
