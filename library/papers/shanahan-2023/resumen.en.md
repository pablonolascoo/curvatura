This comment replies to Cheng and Read, who use "fishbowl universes" where influences propagate at the speed of sound to argue that the absence of a medium makes the speed of light fundamental. The author maintains that the Lorentz transformation simulated there is not Einstein's but that of Lorentz and Poincare, which did assume a medium. He argues these analogues are too successful and show the viability of Lorentzian relativity, with a wave-theoretic treatment of particles that would explain the principle of relativity.
